## What ships

The existing Base now separates original inputs, controllable auxiliary choices and actual outputs; consumes new theorems across known proof-backed representations and monotone completions; and connects fixed or finite conditional plans to every original input. Assembly and verification consume these contracts explicitly. Ten Base skills and five solving stages remain.

The Hamilton plugin gains detailed Q7 controlled donors/return bridges, exact source and compatible-pool counting, and marked multi-omission forest checking. The old Q6 root route remains usable. The selected portable archive has 204 unchanged original files (43,360,734 bytes), including all 178 direct inputs of the two frozen consumers and the complete target04 certificate. The other five new T63 binary families and the full historical archive are not distributed in this selection.

README, current-version navigation, the existing animation and the new bilingual Q7 reader are updated. Q7 remains one research thread in the existing Hamilton domain; the six-domain organization is unchanged. The original research used v3 local adaptation, and feeds methods into v5. Its theorem covers every matching with at least57 edges; the56-edge layer has complete μ=2/4 and several μ=3 families, while fullQ7/P7/seven-direction extension remains open.
