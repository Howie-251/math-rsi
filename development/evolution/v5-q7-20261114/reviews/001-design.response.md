### 2. 三项 Base 展示标题 + 一句解释（中英各一套）

**中文**：
1. **可控辅助选择与依赖后继**：区分任意原输入、构造可控的辅助选择与受其约束的实际输出，先通过辅助选择迫出所需的下游关系性质，避免过早放宽为不必要的全称子问题。
2. **跨已知表示与单调补全的即时消去**：新定理通过验证后，先在本任务已知且有回推证明的合法表示与单调补全上认领覆盖，仅对仍未覆盖的真实余域派发后续任务。
3. **一致固定计划与有限条件计划族**：同时支持对整条实现纤维生效的单固定计划，以及按同一实际输入择一覆盖、且失败条件合取不相容的有限条件计划族。

**English**:
1. **Controlled Auxiliary Choices and Dependent Outputs**: Separate arbitrary original inputs from construction-controlled choices and their induced outputs, using auxiliary choices to enforce downstream relational properties before relaxing to universal subproblems.
2. **Immediate Reduction Across Known Representations and Completions**: Once a theorem is verified, consume its coverage across the task's known proof-backed representations and monotone completions before dispatching work on the residual domain.
3. **Uniform Fixed Plans and Finite Conditional Plan Families**: Support both single plans valid across an entire realization fiber and finite conditional plan sets whose failure conditions cannot jointly hold on any valid original input.
