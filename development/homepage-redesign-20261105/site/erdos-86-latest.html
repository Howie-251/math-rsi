Asymptotic density Q∞: exact rationals T3 and T4

Both unconditional bounds improve the earlier v3 certificate (< 0.59155154) and the 0.60318 reference; 8-digit 0.59124059 is a downward truncation and is never used as an upper bound.

  • Theorem T3 (Q₄ flag algebra + 35 triangle-free COP cuts + 29 cubic terms): exact rational 681653995482306581 / 1152921504606846976 ≈ 0.5912405942282728… ≤ 0.5912405943.
  • Theorem T4 (adding the m = 1024 generalized hypergeometric vertex-degree potential): exact rational 9701859764505653085708308080340303 / 16409326186106551516455239680000000 ≈ 0.5912405941884453… ≤ 0.5912405942.

Five-line unconditional hypergeometric tower for T4 (all n ≥ 1024): (1) Sampling a uniform Q₄ face F ⊆ Qₙ and uniform v ∈ V(F) has the exact same joint law as sampling uniform v ∈ V(Qₙ), uniform S ∈ C([n], 1024), and uniform T ∈ C(S, 4) spanning F through v. (2) The local degree degG[F](v) depends only on (v, T). (3) Conditioning on (v, S) with retained degree dS(v) ∈ {0,…,1024}, hypergeometric expectation gives E[∑k=04 bk 1deg=k | v, S] = Φ1024(dS(v)) / C(1024, 4) ≥ μ1024 / C(1024, 4). (4) Summing over the 16 vertices of F by linearity of expectation requires no independence and holds identically for every n ≥ 1024. (5) Standard O(1/n) flag sampling collision error on Qₙ yields T4 unconditionally.

Coevolution loop in cube_coevolve: empirical survival never substitutes for full-domain proof.

In Q₈, the coevolution engine separates the open proof goal (≤ 682) from the adversarial search target (683) and focuses parent selection on a near-extremal band (≥ 680 edges) while testing candidate inequalities against every archived graph. Surviving obligations feed Theorem B, which couples three probability measures on the same candidate subgraph H ⊆ Q₈ (uniform marked Q₄ completions, 3-degree edge incidences and global vertex degrees), yielding exact rational margin 782808475930428709 / 4209067950080000000000 > 0 and integer maximum −37440 across all 13,150,453 legal marked Q₄ completions.

What v6 learns

  1. Run primal–dual coevolution with explicit goal/target separation, rotating signed residuals and full-archive refutation.
  2. Stop float SDP/CP outer loops when apparent gains fall below repair residuals; record nonzero residuals beside any approximate fit.
  3. Couple local and global observables on the same finite candidate graph before invoking subcube imports.
