How the constructions work

D12: preserve the source, add legal attachments. The 42,402-vertex source stays as an exact induced subgraph. Thirty-one insertions each use 12 permitted attachment points, adding 372 edges in total. The source supplies old–old distances; new-point checks cover the remaining pairs.

D11: connect the geometric core and its boundary. Because the core changes, core–core distances use the H9 geometric theorem under its stated σ/π identification, while the 160 peripheral vertices and the newly added vertex are checked directly against the whole final graph.

Joint completion and verified prefixes

Starting from an inclusion-minimal core-blocking set, attachments are completed jointly across all remaining degree ports. Actual bad vertex pairs guide edits to earlier choices, and each stage builds only on independently verified prefixes.

Verification scope

Both final edge lists regenerate deterministically and pass independent degree and adjacency checks. D12 retains all source edges and runs complete breadth-first search from all 31 new vertices; D11 combines the H9 theorem (under the stated σ/π identification) for core–core pairs with complete breadth-first search from all 161 peripheral and new vertices.
