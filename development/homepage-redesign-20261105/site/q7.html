What is established

  • All matchings with at least 58 edges: complete dense layers (E12) and algebraic construction tools.
  • All matchings with at least 57 edges: the 14-hole assembly (E14) and denser layers form the v5 theorem.
  • All matchings with at least 56 edges (complete E16): adjacent-hole augmentation plus all five independent 16-hole parity-gap classes μ ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3, 4} are closed (parity/weight bounds rule out μ ≥ 5). Whole-proof audit: PASS · same-family · provisional · sound-modulo-imports.

Here μ is the minimum absolute parity-hole difference across the seven coordinate splits. Structure classes are not equal fractions of progress.

Still open
Even hole layers 18–38 (matchings with ≤55 edges), the full Q₇ conjecture, free-root paths P₇ and seven-direction lifting in Qₙ remain open.

Why the method changed

A compressed auxiliary pairing can fall outside the old P6 theorem's input domain. Controlled donor pairings (v5) force the actual successor to meet the next proof step, and return bridges restore all prescribed edges.

To finish the hardest 56-edge remainder (v6), candidate splits were probed on the hardest open branch before adoption rather than dispatching easy sub-slices first. New tools combine a parameterized Q₆ Δ=2 double bridge, the 41-orbit Q₆ A₈ theorem, joint even/odd CNF+DRUP pairs for μ=3, and S₆ large-face independent-hole tables (189 rows for μ=0, 23 rows for μ=1) paired with symbolic small-face holes after dropping five incompatible inner-axis ordering constraints (100,055 − 5 + 1 = 100,051 constraints).

What v5 and v6 learn

  1. Control auxiliary choices and reuse theorems across legal splits before dispatching residual work (v5).
  2. Gate new classifications by bounded probes on the hardest residual branch, and measure net-new union coverage on the same actual input (v6).
  3. Remove incompatible symmetry-breaking constraints whenever one side of a joint model is anchored to an orbit basis while the other side stays symbolic (v6).

Q₆ remains available

Every matching of Q₆ extends to a Hamilton cycle. With imported results, matchings using at most six directions in any dimension also extend.

Evidence and reading

During v6 maintenance, all 261 in-repo E16 files and all 290 bundled baseline imports passed byte and SHA-256 verification; the 189-row μ=0 large-face table and four zero-dependency geometry/plan checkers (MU0 a=0 census and plans, MU0 a=1,2 orbits and Q₆ A₈ 41-orbit census) ran cleanly alongside the v5 S3/S2_STRONG, T63 target04 and Q₆ A10 checks.

Ten large E16 .cnf/.drup traces (532.49 MB, up to 201.55 MB even.drup) are omitted uniformly for normal-Git packaging and recorded in INVENTORY.json; hash manifests do not substitute for DRAT-trim replay from the external R-S-n=7(2).rar archive.
