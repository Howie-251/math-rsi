Theorem schematic.

What is established

  • All matchings with at least 58 edges: complete dense layers and construction tools.
  • All matchings with at least 57 edges: the 14-hole assembly and the denser layers form one complete theorem.
  • At 56 edges: the μ=2 and μ=4 structure classes and several μ=3 families are complete.

Here μ is the minimum absolute parity-hole difference across the seven coordinate splits. Structure classes are not equal fractions of progress.

Still open
The full Q₇ conjecture, P7 and seven-direction extension remain open. The complete 56-edge layer is not closed.

Why the method changed

A compressed auxiliary pairing can fall outside the old P6 theorem's input domain. The new constructions control a legal donor pairing so the actual successor has the interface needed by the next proof step. Return bridges then restore the original prescribed edges.

Exact source-fiber and compatible-pool counts guide finite generation. New family theorems are used across known legal coordinate splits before more work is assigned. A fixed universal plan, or a finite plan set selected from the actual matching, covers every realization in its stated family.

What v5 learns

  1. Control auxiliary choices to make the next proof step possible.
  2. Apply new theorems across known legal representations before assigning more work.
  3. Cover every input with fixed or conditional finite plans.

v5 deepens the Hamilton plugin and inherits the existing v4 global reassessment.

Q₆ remains available

Every matching of Q₆ extends to a Hamilton cycle. With imported results, matchings using at most six directions in any dimension also extend.

Evidence and reading

During v5 maintenance, the T63 target04 check reconstructed all 4,096² connectivity bits and obtained rank 4,096. The legacy Q6 A10 interface passed its full 1,024² check. Both relocated S3 and S2_STRONG family-plan consumers passed from repository inputs.

The other five T63 targets were not rerun. These finite checks retain their imported rank and expansion premises; they do not replace whole-proof acceptance. The two family domains overlap and are not added into aggregate coverage.
