Happy Ending · Erdős–Szekeres & ordered rank-3 signotopes

Restricted capacities N(m,r,s) and planar thresholds

Path-avoiding signotope capacities across m = 7, 8, 9 and general m, combining replayed CNF+DRAT and native-trace integer dual certificates with structural working proofs.

Run: pre-MathRSI Trellis + ARIS (through Nov 3) and Trellis + ARIS + MathRSI v3 (Nov 5–17) → integrated in v6 main maintenance.

Objects and three-step assembly

On a complete ordered rank-3 signotope (every 4-tuple packet changes sign at most once), N(m,r,s) is the maximum number of elements avoiding blue r-vertex paths, red s-vertex paths and same-endpoint weak m-gons, while ES(m) is the planar general-position threshold forcing a convex m-gon. Write Cₘ = N(m,m−1,m−1), Qₘ = N(m,m−1,m), Fₘ = N(m,m,m), and let Dₘ denote the R7 residual capacity with junction exclusion (no element simultaneously ends a red (m−2)-path and starts a blue (m−3)-path; Dₘ cannot substitute for unconstrained N(m,m−2,m)). For m ≥ 6, disjoint H–R splitting (R7) gives Qₘ ≤ Cₘ + Dₘ, leftmost-pivot chart transport (R20) gives Fₘ = Qₘ + 1, and planar inclusion adds 1: ES(m) ≤ Fₘ + 1 ≤ Cₘ + Dₘ + 2.

m = 8 · shared-label CEGAR

N(8,6,6) ≤ 61

In the 4 × 4 box of 70 states, 63 six-order box chains and 30 shared-pivot rows give 93 baseline rows (≤ 62). Adding 11 DRAT-verified shared-label CEGAR kernels (plus transposes = 115 rows) makes budget 8 UNSAT, forcing at least 9 holes.

A 9-hole hitting vector intersects all 115 rows, proving this matrix relaxation peaks at 9 (a matrix witness, not a 61-point signotope).

m = 9 · native boundary traces

ES(9) ≤ 1148

Self-contained integer duals (Λ = 10⁶) prove ≥ 236 holes in 924 states (C₉ = N(9,8,8) ≤ 688) and ≥ 124 holes in 582 restricted states (D₉ ≤ 458, where a rational vector of sum 123,539,833 / 10⁶ ≤ 124 satisfies all 47,635 depth-≤3 rows).

R7 and R20 assembly gives working bounds Q₉ ≤ 1146, F₉ ≤ 1147 and ES(9) ≤ 1148, below Mojarrad–Vlachos (2016) 1508 and the 1501* (= 924 + 575 + 2) planar reference.

Heptagon and octagon structure notes

  • Heptagons (m = 7): Owner-interval summation rules out 40 points to give N(7,5,7) ≤ 39 without CNF, and alternating endpoint-cycle exclusion gives N(7,6,6) ≤ 49. Because known global interfaces still take min{3b, C + b + 5, A + C − 1, ⌊9A/4⌋, ⌊9C/5⌋} = 75 at b = 25, pushing past N(7,6,7) ≤ 75 turns to joint transversal cost: DRAT verification rules out 5 ordered common lock blocks (at most 4 blocks), and any 74-point candidate must carry at least 5 clean roots in one global port flow.
  • Octagons (m = 8): Coordinate-slice propagation from N(8,4,8) = 22 (Baek) and N(8,6,6) ≤ 61 yields working bounds N(8,5,7) ≤ 51, N(8,5,8) ≤ 66, N(8,6,7) ≤ 109 and N(8,6,8) ≤ 165. Replaying Schroeder’s 15-file public finite omission trees confirms C₈ ≤ 193 and D₈ ≤ 124; paired with our cyclic-sign packet bridge (pending independent review), this lifts to signotope N(8,8,8) ≤ 318, while planar ES(8) ≤ 319 remains Schroeder’s published preprint bound.

Assurance boundaries & open targets
Five finite suites (native-trace 688 & 458 + 47,635-row ceiling, milestone9 N(8,6,6) ≤ 61 CNF+DRAT, Schroeder’s 15-file omission trees, m = 7 five-lock-block DRAT, and the 25-point E21 abstract witness) are replayed directly in-repo. All structural, cyclic-sign transfer, and R7/R20 assembly manuscripts remain working proofs pending independent mathematical review, and there is no Lean formalization. Unconditional N(7,6,7) ≤ 74, whether N(8,6,6) drops below 61, and depth-≥4 or shared-label improvements beyond D₉ ≤ 458 remain open.
