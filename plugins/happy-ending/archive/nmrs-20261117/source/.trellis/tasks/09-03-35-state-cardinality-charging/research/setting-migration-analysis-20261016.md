# Migration of the state-charging setting beyond (7,5,6)

Date: 2026-10-16. Scope: feasibility of an isolated research prototype.
The active 756 production encoding, its accepted results, and running campaigns
are preserved. The accompanying probe supplies engineering evidence only.

## Conclusion from the mathematical and source audit

The terminal-pair state method and exact occupancy/counting framework extend.
The present production API does not already implement that extension.
A separate parameter-aware target/state interface is necessary.

DownsetSpec currently requires (a-1)+(b-1)=n+2, while the independent
extractor equates exceptionality with one simultaneous pair of maximal paths.
The legacy relaxed wrapper etv_signotope_extension also enforces this same
identity through _validate_endpoint_query_parameters. For n=7 this restricts the intended
setting to a+b=11. Replacing 5/6 by larger bounds without changing these
semantics would either reject the input or miss relevant 7-gon witnesses.

For W=a-2,H=b-2, a state is a nonincreasing W-tuple with entries in [0,H].
There are binomial(W+H,W) states. The empty state is ordinary. Nonempty
states with bounding-box dimensions w,h number binomial(w+h-2,w-1).
Consequently O is 1 plus the sum of this quantity over
1<=w<=W,1<=h<=H,w+h<=6; E=binomial(W+H,W)-O.

The table describes catalogs, not realizability of every state in a strict
model, and does not establish any new extremal bound.

## What carries over

Let sigma(u,v) be the two longest monochromatic terminal-pair path lengths
minus one, and D(v) the down-set generated by sigma(t,v), t<v.
For u<v, sigma(u,v) is in D(v). For every t<u the color of (t,u,v)
extends one path ending at (t,u), so at least one coordinate of sigma(u,v)
strictly exceeds that of sigma(t,u). Hence sigma(u,v) is not in D(u).
Thus D(v) is not a subset of D(u), and all endpoint states are distinct.
This proof uses complete coloring and exact path recurrence, not the
particular 756 dimensions or a previously established C_r theorem.

It follows that M=O+e-m, where e counts occupied exceptional TYPES and m
counts missing ordinary TYPES. Exact cells m=r-1 therefore have
M=O+1+e-r. Ordinary and exceptional are derived from the current catalog.

Bridges also generalize: a cell (x,y) in D(u) has a common terminal
predecessor w<u. The color of (w,u,v) yields a blue terminal path of length
x+2 or a red terminal path of length y+2. A term reaching a forbidden length
is false. The dimensions in the loop and boundary tests must be dynamic.

F3-main ranks use the stronger non-subset observation above. If endpoint v
has state h, occupied proper subsets of h must occur before v, and occupied
proper supersets after v. Their counts are bounded by v-1 and M-v.
Exact counter definitions give the intended auxiliary extension. This
argument is independent of the ordinary/exceptional partition.

## What must change or be reproved

A vertex is exceptional when its bounding-box width plus height is at least
7. Equivalently it realizes at least one feasible split pair in the table.
For a weak 7-gon, both paths for the SAME pair must have the SAME outer
left and right endpoints. Different pairs and starts cannot be combined.

For example, in the 757 box, (4,4,4) is exceptional without filling the
3 x 5 box. On (5,5,5), deleting one column or one row still leaves an
exceptional state. Thus the old full-box interpretation and one-step
ordinary-shadow arguments cannot simply be reused.

The single-pair endpoint orientation, first-event, RTQ, Qlt/Qeq/Qgt and
typed-Hall interfaces are intentionally outside this feasibility extension.
Their mathematics and schemas require explicit multi-pair redesign.

The established 756 C_r results quantify over a stricter class of
colorings. Increasing a or b admits additional colorings; it does not
transport those UNSAT results or their charging inequalities to the new
class. Every new claim needs new target-specific obligations and evidence.

## A tractable campaign shape to consider after validation

If the objective is the numerical implication M<=O, heredity allows one
to exclude only order M=O+1. The exact counting identity partitions that
order by e and m=e-1. The empty state D(1) is always occupied, so m<=O-1,
and only 1<=e<=min(E,O) is needed.

| Setting | Fixed order O+1 | Exact spine cells | All positive-defect (e,m) count pairs before other pruning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 756 | 26 | 10 | 55 |
| 757 | 27 | 26 | 455 |
| 766 | 31 | 30 | 765 |
| 767 | 32 | 31 | 2480 |
| 777 | 33 | 32 | 6544 |

The final column is L(E+1)-L(L+1)/2 with L=min(E,O); it counts
e>=m+1, 0<=m<=O-1, e<=E, and does not assert model realizability.
The spine is sufficient for this particular numerical goal, but does not
replace the stronger all-order C_r hierarchy. Restriction must recompute
states from the restricted coloring; an old occupancy mask is not hereditary.

These orders are proposed test targets, not established upper bounds.
Start with 757 and 766; retain the exact+bridges+F3 method with fresh parameter,
catalog, formula, source and proof bindings. Do not launch the full expanded
C_r lattice by mechanically extending the old 55-cell schedule.

## Completed Linux engineering evidence

The prototype and default-profile probes completed on the authoritative Linux
server in forktqiu3. Raw evidence is in
[setting-migration-20261016](setting-migration-20261016/).

- [43 initial checks](setting-migration-20261016/semantics.json) passed:
  all 1024 order-5 colorings for each of five settings (5120 setting/coloring
  combinations); all 16 order-4 colorings per setting with canonical exact
  CNF/bridges/F3 extension (80 combinations); catalog/count/invalid-input
  checks; 896 valid 756 profile comparisons against the production extractor.
  This baseline comparison is catalog/state semantics, not a complete
  equivalence certification against the production CNF.
- [15 external AE solves](setting-migration-20261016/solver_results.json):
  five strict order-8 searches requiring an exceptional endpoint; five
  exact (e,m) rebuilds with those audited colorings fixed; one intentionally
  omitted-split SAT control; four UNSAT negative controls with actual
  successful repaired proof checks. All five strict models have e=2 and
  m=19,20,24,25,26 respectively. There are no timeouts.
- The four checked negative controls cover the omitted target split,
  reverse state definition, reverse occupancy definition, and deliberately
  wrong exact counts while retaining the order/count identity. These are
  small semantic tests, not full extremal problem proofs.
- [Supplementary checks](setting-migration-20261016/supplementary_semantics.json)
  replay a known strict exceptional order-7 coloring with e=1 in all five
  settings, reject partial/non-Boolean assignments and an unsafe one-way
  occupancy interpretation, and verify deterministic formula bytes.
- A fresh Sol/high engineering checker added
  [12 geometric pair controls](setting-migration-20261016/engineering_pair_controls.json),
  one per admissible setting/pair. Each validates nondegenerate geometry,
  signotope packets and brute paths; the full target rejects its canonical
  assignment while omitting its sole active pair permits it. These use
  bridges with F3 disabled explicitly and invoke no solver.

The 777 formula is 221,581,580 DIMACS bytes. Its exact core has 6256 variables;
F3 adds 2,449,602. Therefore the current full-threshold F3 construction
dominates growth already at tiny order. Production migration should address
this scaling before launching larger-order workloads. No optimization or
performance improvement is claimed by this feasibility experiment.

All 15 solver calls total 7.07024 seconds. The complete solver phase, including
construction/export/audit/check work, took 98.71 seconds; /usr/bin/time reported
peak RSS 3,590,192 KiB. These easy order-8 SAT timings do not forecast hard
orders 27/31/32/33 counterexample searches or their proof sizes.

The [fresh engineering check](setting-migration-20261016/ENGINEERING_CHECK.md)
found no blocking defect in the tested scope. It independently replayed all
11 SAT assignments against the actual DIMACS clauses including assumptions,
checked the 15 run/file bindings and four successful checker records, and
performed the 12 geometric pair controls. It did not rerun the proof checker
or substitute for an independent scientific review.
