## Present implication and next mathematical need

The pure unit-eight and pure unit-nine families have not separately closed. The existing total degree and center-codegree budgets guarantee simultaneous large pools, but do not yet contradict their common realizability. In particular the complete two-neighbor gate still allows codegree one between a unit-eight and a unit-nine point, in either central block relation. The forest restriction concerns unit-eight pairs and gives no immediate lower bound above one on these cross pairs. Treating both pure pools as if their worst inventories were mutually incompatible would be unjustified.

A useful next argument must couple these two actual pools through the same old edge slots or genuine mixed inclusive-count identities, or derive a stronger selection rule from the global girth condition. No such new contradiction is claimed here.
