# Bounded primary-source check for the nine-positive signed gate

**Result:** this bounded search did not locate a published theorem or explicit counterexample deciding the proposed gate. None of the checked results justifies assuming it is satisfiable. Conversely, the general hardness results do not supply a counterexample with its precise parameters.

The target is a clause-monotone CNF with exactly nine all-positive clauses of length four, t≤3 all-negative clauses of length three, b all-negative clauses of length four, t+b≤20, at most18 variables and total variable occurrence at most7. “Monotone” here permits both signs globally but requires each individual clause to use one sign. The positive/negative clause counts are not per-variable literal-occurrence bounds.

## What is still needed

No checked source controls simultaneously nine positive four-clauses, the joint negative budget t+b≤20, total occurrence7 and at most18 variables. In particular, none gives a sign-count-sensitive minimal-unsatisfiable lower bound excluding this exact class. Those actual resources therefore still require the parent proof or an explicit verified signed counterexample. A PropertyB lower bound concerns both colors on the same hyperedge family and is not, without a separate reduction, a theorem for these two unrelated signed families.
