## Restricted five-lock tail: root's selection repair

Root subsequently supplied structural selection lemmas that avoid the exact failed five-lock traces. The earlier unrestricted failures are retained above as diagnostic history; under the new actual selection, the tail v42–44 is a complete local candidate.

At v42, choose five degree-9 unit locks with no single original edge containing all five. The at least 24 low-degree vertices and conflict maximum degree 4 justify the initial five locks. If they lie in one edge E, at least 18 low-degree vertices lie outside E. The five locks have at most 20 conflict incidences to outside vertices, so some outside y has at most one conflict with the five. Four nonneighbors together with y give five unit locks. They cannot all lie in another original edge F, since that would make a pair among the four old locks belong to both E and F, contradicting unit codegree.

At v43 and44, the low-degree conflict graph supplies at least nine unit locks S0. If an original edge E contains at least four of S0, it contains at most six; choose three of E∩S0 and two of S0\E. Every other edge F contains at most one chosen E-point by unit codegree, so its selected trace has size at most three. E also has selected trace size three. If no edge has trace at least four in S0, any five-point subset already has the required property. Thus five degree-9 unit locks can be chosen with no four- or five-point trace. Root owns the final structural proof, with separate independent checking requested.
