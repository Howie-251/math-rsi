## Uniform fourteen-hole source transfer

Fix any realizing original short M, target-facing cut A and complementary face B. Put a=|Z intersect A|, b=|W intersect A|, and delta=1 if root0 belongs to A.

In even consumption require b>=1 and s=a+1-b>=0. Choose S of size s from Z intersect A. In odd consumption put s=b-a-1>=1 and choose S from W intersect A excluding a free target and the root mirror. In either case Y is the set of selected source mirrors. The code checks the exact source pool, count and original-mirror nonhole condition.

The final B matching has actual marked shape:

- even: minority k-a-1=6-a, majority k-b+s=7-b+s, omissions Y of size s;
- odd: minority k-b=7-b, majority k-a-1+s=6-a+s, omissions Y of size s.

Here majority is simply the branch's designated local parity class; every accepted imported shape has that exact orientation and count. Actual sets, not just those counts, are looked up.

For even consumption choose local root u and pair X_A union S when delta=1; when delta=0 choose r in S and pair X_A union(S minus r). For odd consumption choose root u and pair X_A union S when delta=1; when delta=0 choose a free even port r and pair (X_A minus r) union S. The latter port exists by surplus b-a=s+1. The port identity |X_A intersect E|-|X_A intersect O|=b-a-delta makes every completion set even. All completion vertices are free for M_A and exclude the local root and any free target.

The completed matching has balanced b:b holes in even consumption and (a+1):(a+1) holes in odd consumption. The accepted FULL Q5 arbitrary-matching endpoint theorem applies for every feasible balanced count, not merely the old small b/a ranges. Artificial completion edges may be arbitrary. The local root remains free/even, target remains odd and may be free or covered. Feasibility follows from the actual matching, not an unproved hole-count extrapolation. Zero crossings use the declared five-direction endpoint input.

Delete completion edges D from the A Hamilton path, attach actual crossings and selected bridges, add the tagged root--target edge, and compress the resulting forest. With root in A and positive crossings, every used source count is at least2 and parity forces D nonempty; the tagged A cycle is cut. With root in B, attachments are distinct B leaves, including its tag leaf. The covered target-port boundary is allowed: deletion can isolate target v in A, but crossing and tag restore degree two. A free target cannot lie in D.

Each y in Y is originally internally B-covered: its free A mirror excludes crossing coverage, nonroot independence excludes another original hole, and legal sources exclude the root. Its distinct original short mate has parity opposite Y, outside Y/ports/free root. Only those pendant conflicts occur, so simultaneous suppression cannot make a conflict cycle. The actual L is a perfect matching on B minus H_*, H_*=(H intersect B minus root) union Y. This proves the marked counts and sets used by the scanner. Applying an accepted universal local cycle on exactly B minus Y, expanding all paths and deleting the tag gives the specified Hamilton path, preserving every original edge and only short new physical edges.

## Forced-classification hypothesis discharge

Each forced registry row supplies a single actual pair pq of covered B vertices with parity Y, and local cycle existence iff that pair is absent from arbitrary L. Its source count is3 or4, within the accepted generic forced-cherry theorem's s>=3 scope. The scanner transports pq with the same automorphism as the marked sets and rejects root-in-obstruction witnesses. Root0 is in B exactly when the target's cut bit is1; its projection then is0, so the actual forced mask must omit bit0 in that case.

Consequently the generic root-not-OB theorem applies to every actual original matching: an unchanged internal pair cannot produce the same-parity obstruction; suppressed source mates have the opposite parity; if both obstruction endpoints are ports, prescribe their actual A crossing-partner pair inside D and keep the automatic spare deletion gap. This excludes pq from the **actual compressed L**, not just original M_B. The unknown target mate is never chosen favorably. Root-in-pair records are not accepted through this forced route even if a different proof might solve them.

The new root-mirror-omitted author transfer and A mixed-opening ideas are not used. STAR_B10 is used solely as its accepted local forced classification and combined with the already accepted generic root-not-OB transfer in this different14-hole application.
