   these holes would assign every vertex of S to a distinct member of T,
   which is impossible. This excludes realizability of that marked hole
   geometry by an original short matching; it is not a failed Hamilton
   search and does not assert a negative path-extension result.

and requires EXACT equality with T and strict deficiency. No max-matching
routine, producer failure result or sufficiency half of Hall's theorem
is needed for this exclusion argument.

subtraction or an assumption that a sufficient extension class must be
