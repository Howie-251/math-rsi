In the odd branch the explicit exclusions remove the free target and the root mirror; with root in B there are s+1 more even than odd A ports, so a free even port root exists. Ports and sources are disjoint. All V_D have even size and omit the local root and any free target. A covered target may become a D endpoint when it is a port, which is allowed by the imported endpoint theorem.

3. **All-balanced endpoint import.** The remaining hole counts are exactly (b,b) or (a+1,a+1), including the free even root. Counts are feasible because they arise from an actual matching on 32 vertices. The target remains odd and legal, whether free or covered. Artificial D edges may be arbitrary, so prescribed same-parity crossing-partner pairs do not violate the import. The final A10 acceptance explicitly includes all feasible balanced counts, including the empty-matching endpoint boundary.

4. **Deletion size.** With delta=1, d=(c+s)/2>=2. With delta=0, d=(c+s-1)/2>=2; in the odd case c>=s+1 gives d>=s. Parity removes the possible half-integral boundary. These estimates hold independently of k.

5. **Original mirror mates.** Every y in Y is internally M_B-covered. Its free mirror excludes crossing coverage; nonroot independence excludes an original opposite-parity hole; parity or the explicit root-mirror exclusion excludes u. The short mates h_y are distinct, have parity opposite Y, and cannot be ports, free root, or Y. Hence the only T/M_B conflicts are the stated pendant conflicts. This step genuinely uses shortness of the original M, not shortness of L.

6. **Tagged transferred forest.** For delta=1, P_A+uv is a simple 32-cycle: uv cannot already be an edge of a 32-vertex Hamilton path whose endpoints are u,v. Removing nonempty D gives a forest. For delta=0, a path with distinct B leaves, including the root tag leaf, stays a forest. The root, ports, and mirrors are distinct B endpoints. After attachments every A degree is two; thus compression is a perfect matching T of B endpoints with exactly one tagged edge.

7. **Isolated target port.** If a covered target is a port and loses its unique P_A edge, the retained tag and crossing give it degree two. With root in A this is already in the tagged-cycle argument. With root in B the segment is u-v-bar(v); its root endpoint cannot lie in an R cycle. A free target never lies in D. Thus the proof does not incorrectly assume the A forest has no isolated vertices.

8. **Suppression and final holes.** T endpoint conflicts at Y consist only of paths with distinct pendant h_y; no conflict component can close. Suppressing all Y gives L on exactly K, with holes (H∩B) minus {u}, union Y. The stated global parity hole counts follow. The true local input must be applied to this actual L, not to the original M_B.

9. **Same-parity L edges.** If endpoints are in V(R), both are originally covered, because root is excluded. A nonport endpoint retains an original short edge to the opposite parity; that edge is unaffected since its mate cannot lie in Y. Suppression only moves a Y endpoint to an opposite-parity h_y. An edge to u cannot lie wholly in V(R). Thus every L edge wholly in V(R) is an unchanged T edge between two ports. This argument applies to all edges on a multi-R alternating cycle, including a parallel two-edge cycle.

10. **Prescribed J_A is legal.** Both-port R pairs are disjoint. In the even branch the root is u or a source, never a port. In the odd branch with root in B, R's B endpoints are even, their A crossing partners are odd, and the chosen port root is even. With root in A it is free u. All prescribed endpoints therefore lie in V_D, disjointly. The remaining vertices can be paired arbitrarily.

11. **Single-cherry separation.** Deleting a prescribed D edge from a path separates its endpoints. From the tagged A cycle, deletion of that edge and at least one other D edge cuts both complementary arcs. Distinct B leaves cannot reconnect components. Hence its B pair is absent from T, and therefore L. This proof quantifies over every local endpoint path delivered for the prescribed D.
