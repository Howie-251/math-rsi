## A simultaneous three-omission input

For rows $(4,2)$ and $(1,5)$ the uniform transfer needs a Q5 cycle for
six same-parity holes, omitting the three actual marked mirror holes.
The required matching is arbitrary after compression.

A promising sufficient criterion cuts Q5 into faces with 2/4 holes and
1/2 marked omissions. A nonempty crossing matching allows transfer using
Q4 N14 and Q4 T44. To ensure some such cut has a crossing edge for every
matching, one can inspect the cells determined by all admissible cuts:
an odd number of covered vertices in a cell forbids every edge staying
within all those cells.

`ten_covered_t63_split_profile.py` checks every actual bridge choice in
the 54222 residual geometries. That particular odd-cell criterion covers
zero of them. The script preserves every record in its residual output.
It does not disprove the Q5 input or rule out the positive-crossing case
for individual original matchings. A treatment of zero crossing, or a
different split, is required for useful uniform coverage here.
