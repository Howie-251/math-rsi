# Odd abelian normal subgroups of transitive target groups are free C3 or C5

**status:** `verified`

## Statement
For a transitive automorphism group of a 60-point target, every nontrivial abelian normal p-group for p=3 or5 is semiregular of order p. An odd abelian normal subgroup is C3,C5 or C15; the previous four-free-Z15 exclusion removes C15.

## Honest scope
Requires a transitive group and an abelian normal subgroup. Does not assert that every vertex-transitive target has such a subgroup. C15 uses an existing finite-family exclusion.
