# A free C5 target60 needs at least two independent orbits

**status:** `verified`

## Statement
Any order60 target invariant under an automorphism of cycle type5^12 has at least two independent vertex orbits. Its off-diagonal quotient support must contain a triangle. In particular all voltage assignments for quotient2I+A(K6,6) are impossible.

## Honest scope
Every target for a free C5 action, without vertex transitivity or normality. Does not exclude the full C5 domain with two or more independent orbits.
