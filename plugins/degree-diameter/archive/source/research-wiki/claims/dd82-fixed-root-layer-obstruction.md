# Fixed-root Petersen construction requires same-layer outer edges

**status:** `verified`

## Statement
In the normalized fixed-A Petersen model, outer matchings cannot all exchange the two coordinate layers. A valid graph has an even number of same-layer outer edges, between2 and24.
