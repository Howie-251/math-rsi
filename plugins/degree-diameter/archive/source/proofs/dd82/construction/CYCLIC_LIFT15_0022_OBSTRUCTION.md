# Four Z15 orbits: the 0022 construction obstruction

Date:2026-10-11. This is a construction-family exclusion, not an arbitrary order60 exclusion. All set convolutions below count multiplicities. No Lean claim is made.

## Complete quotient screen

For a free cyclic action with four orbits of length15, internal neighbor sets A_i are inverse-closed and exclude0, so their sizes a_i belong to{0,2,4,6,8}. Every target is8-regular. Relabel to a_0≤a_1≤a_2≤a_3 and write c_ij for cross-set sizes. Set d_i=8−a_i. Every nonnegative integer cross matrix is generated by

    c01=x, c02=y, c03=d0−x−y,
    c12=(d0+d1+d2−d3)/2−x−y,
    c13=d1−x−c12, c23=d2−y−c12,

with0≤x,y≤8. Same-orbit coverage has upper bound

    min(15,1+a_i+a_i²/2) + sum_(j!=i) min(14,c_ij(c_ij−1)).

Cross-orbit coverage has upper bound

    c_ij(1+a_i+a_j) + sum_(k!=i,j) c_ik c_kj.

Each must be at least15. The first uses the commutative inverse-pair sum bound; the second allows overlap and so is a necessary bound. The [exact screen](../../../computations/dd82/construction/cyclic_lift15_quotient_screen.py) and [result](../../../computations/dd82/construction/cyclic_lift15_quotient_screen.json) leave seven labeled matrices and three types under orbit relabeling:

1. 0000, cross sizes3,3,2 opposite-paired;
2. 0022, c01=2,c23=0 and c02=c03=c12=c13=3;
3. 2222, c01=c23=0 and the other four cross sizes3.

Other companion notes treat types1 and3. This document proves type2 impossible, with no further finite search.

## Exact type0022 obligations

Write U=C01={0,u} after a coordinate translation, X=C02, Y=C03, Z=C12, W=C13, all last four of size3. Put A2={±a}, A3={±b} with a,b nonzero. We may represent u,a,b up to sign by1,...,7 when evaluating the equations involving cosines.

For S=δ0+δa+δ−a, the0–2 and1–2 cross capacities are exactly15. Hence complete coverage forces the convolution identities

    X*S + U*Z = J,       Z*S + (−U)*X = J.                 (1)

Orbit0 has no internal edges. Its same-orbit coverage is exactly the union of0, ΔU, ΔX, ΔY, where ΔV=(V−V)\{0}. The capacities1+2+6+6=15 force the three nonzero difference sets to be pairwise disjoint and maximal.

## Primitive-character restriction without numerical trigonometry

Let ζ be a primitive15th root. X(ζ) is nonzero. Otherwise the sum of its three unit complex numbers is zero, so they form an equilateral triangle and X is a coset of{0,5,10}. This would give |ΔX|=2 instead of6 and violate the preceding same-orbit bound.

Eliminate Z in(1). At ζ, where J vanishes, X(ζ)≠0 gives

    (1+ζ^a+ζ^(−a))² = 2+ζ^u+ζ^(−u).                      (2)

Take the field trace from Q(ζ) to Q. For nonzero t mod15 define c15(t)=sum_(gcd(k,15)=1) ζ^(kt). Its values are1,−2,−4 when gcd(t,15) is1,3,5 respectively. Indeed an order5 root occurs twice among the eight embeddings, an order3 root four times; the sum of primitive15th roots is1 by partitioning all15th roots into orders1,3,5,15.

Since multiplication by2 preserves these gcd classes, the trace of(2) is

    24+6 c15(a) = 16+2 c15(u),
    c15(u)=4+3 c15(a).

Among the three possible trace values only c15(a)=c15(u)=−2 is allowed. Thus gcd(a,15)=gcd(u,15)=3. The order5 identity simplifies the left side of(2) to2+ζ^a+ζ^(−a), hence u=±a modulo15. In particular u and a are multiples of3. Applying exactly the same argument to Y,W,b shows b is also a multiple of3.

## The uncovered offsets

Evaluate(1) at a primitive cube root ω. Now S(ω)=3 and U(ω)=(−U)(ω)=2. Thus

    3X(ω)+2Z(ω)=0,       2X(ω)+3Z(ω)=0,

which forces X(ω)=Z(ω)=0. As X has three elements, its counts in the three residue classes modulo3 are equal: exactly one in each. The same argument forces Y to be a modulo3 transversal.

Consequently neither ΔX nor ΔY contains a nonzero multiple of3. There are four such offsets3,6,9,12, while ΔU covers only u and−u. At least two offsets in orbit0 remain at distance greater than2. This contradicts the original diameter condition and excludes the entire stated0022 voltage family.
