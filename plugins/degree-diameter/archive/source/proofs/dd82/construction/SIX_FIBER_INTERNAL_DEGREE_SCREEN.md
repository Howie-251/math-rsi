# Six equal ten-vertex fibers: screening the internal degree

Date: 2026-10-12. This is a necessary-condition classification of a specified
equitable construction family, not an exclusion of arbitrary order 60.

## Model and common identities

Partition a proposed eight-regular diameter-two graph into six fibers of ten
vertices. Assume each fiber induces a d-regular graph, with the same integer
d in all six fibers. Assume every bipartite interface is regular of degree
q_ij at both ends. Thus Q=(q_ij) is symmetric, nonnegative integral, has zero
diagonal, and each row has sum k=8-d. No matching or automorphism assumption
is imposed on an interface.

Write s_i=sum_j q_ij^2. From a fixed vertex in fiber i, the direct and
two-step routes to fiber j number at most

    (1+2d) q_ij + (Q^2)_ij.

Every one of the ten targets must occur, so this is at least ten for i!=j.
Summing over the five other fibers gives

    50 <= (1+2d)k + k^2-s_i = 72-d-d^2-s_i.       (1)

Inside its own fiber, internal radius-two routes cover at most 1+d^2
vertices. An external neighbor in fiber j can return to at most q_ij-1
other vertices of fiber i; therefore

    10 <= 1+d^2+s_i-k,
    17-d-d^2 <= s_i <= 22-d-d^2.                  (2)

These counts are upper bounds on distinct targets. Route coincidences can
only reduce the number reached, so they are valid necessary conditions
without any assumptions about girth or disjointness.

## Internal degree at least four is impossible

Integer nonnegative q gives s_i>=sum_j q_ij=k. Equation (1) implies

    50 <= 64-d^2.

This fails for d>=4. The argument includes all d up to eight and needs no
enumeration.

## Independent fibers (d=0) are impossible by a small complete screen

Here k=8. Equation (2) and s_i congruent to sum_j q_ij modulo two give
s_i in {18,20,22}. Each q_ij<=4 because a value at least five alone has
square at least 25. The only sorted row types are

    (0,0,2,3,3), (0,1,1,2,4), (0,1,1,3,3),
    (0,1,2,2,3), (1,1,1,1,4).

They have 185 distinct permutations in total. All symmetric six-by-six
zero-diagonal matrices with such rows were generated by assigning rows in
order and accepting exactly those matching already assigned columns.
Every matrix satisfying the necessary conditions occurs once. There are
9,900 labeled matrices. **None** satisfies all fifteen additional conditions

    q_ij + sum_k q_ik q_kj >= 10.

Consequently no target with this equitable independent-fiber partition exists.
This conclusion uses the finite computation; it is not labeled purely analytic.

## Remaining degrees and precise scope

- For d=3, the analytic
  [cross-degree rigidity](SIX_FIBER_CROSS_DEGREE_RIGIDITY.md) forces all
  off-diagonal q_ij=1. Within-fiber diameter two then forces each cubic
  ten-vertex fiber to be Petersen.
- For d=2, [degree-two rigidity](SIX_FIBER_DEGREE_TWO_RIGIDITY.md) forces
  Q=2 A(K_3,3). The [double-interface argument](K33_DOUBLE_INTERFACE_MODEL.md)
  regroups any target as six Petersen fibers with single matching interfaces.
- For d=1, the matching-fiber quotient and simultaneous interface obligations
  remain a separate research case. It is not discarded by the conclusions above.

Thus only d=1 and the full six-Petersen family remain within this specified
uniform equitable six-fiber framework. The d=1 case is not claimed to have
an actual diameter-two realization. Unequal fibers, nonuniform internal
degrees, and nonequitable interfaces are outside the model. The mathematical
interval stays 57<=N(8,2)<=61; there is still no legal graph above 57.
