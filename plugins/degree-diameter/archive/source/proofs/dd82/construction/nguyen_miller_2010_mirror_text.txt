Graphs of Diameter 2 and Defect 2
This paper investigates the structural properties of regular graphs with diameter 2 and defect 2, building on previous work by Erdos et al. The authors characterize these graphs and explore configurations of vertex repeats, providing insights into the degree/diameter problem. Key findings include the identification of repeat types and their relationships, which contribute to a deeper understanding of graph structures in this context.
