Results obtained by using a hybrid discovery framework in development: LLM-guided search over classical algebraic constructions, combined with local search and exact completion. The specific LLMs used were OpenAI's Sol 5.6 and Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 Max.
Incidence graph of the split Cayley generalized hexagon H(8) (74898 vertices, 9-regular, diameter 6). Hermitian spread of 513 pairwise opposite lines; the 9 lines of one regulus stay untouched, on each of the other 504 lines 5 of the 9 incident edges are subdivided (2520 new vertices): the points of every such line carry cyclic labels in Z9 (hyperplanes through the 3-space of the fixed regulus) and exactly the points whose label lies in a difference cover of Z9 stay untouched - the largest uniform choice the construction allows. New vertices of one spread line form a clique; each new vertex has 3 further "external" edges. The external edges are invariant under a group of order 504 (isomorphic to SL(2,8)) that acts regularly on the 504 subdivided spread lines and preserves the label structure, so they are given by 8 edge orbits (label, label, group element).
