# Uniform K4 blocks with one external permutation cannot exceed60 controller vertices

## Conclusion and exact domain

Let H be any group of order m. The controller vertices are(i,h), i∈{0,1,2,3},
h∈H. For each fixed h, join the four vertices as a complete K4. Fix one
permutation P of the four indices and four group elements g_0,...,g_3. Its
remaining edges are generated by

    F(i,h)=(P(i),h*g_i)

and the actual inverse F⁻¹. If this controller has diameter at most3, then

    m≤15, hence |V(controller)|=4m≤60.

In particular the proposed64=4×16 controller cannot work for any Z16
voltages, or for any other16-element regular deck group, within this exact
uniform K4/single-F organization. This is a path-count argument, not a group
enumeration or an upper bound on arbitrary64vertex controllers. Nonuniform
external2factors and other controller organizations remain open.

No4096vertex fiber graph, candidate controller or chart assignment was
generated. Every fiber edge in the proposed construction projects to a
controller edge, so a fiber graph of diameter3 cannot repair this controller
obstruction.

## The concrete prediction and earlier unused gate

The initial PRED.json proposed all5 conjugacy types of P and all16⁴ voltage
assignments per type,327680 assignments in total. Internal ports were xor1,
xor2,xor3, external ports F,F⁻¹, inverse ports(0,1,2,4,3). The finite C++ gate
was compiled successfully, but its enumeration binary was never run: actual
numeric voltage assignments evaluated0. It remains as an unexecuted earlier
implementation, not evidence of a numerical search.

Before that enumeration started, the same-index short-route prediction below
gave a stronger obstruction. It was checked by a3120word symbolic test,
0.002083549CPU within the60CPU budget. The five permutation types suffice
because conjugating P by any S4 relabeling preserves the complete K4 blocks
and merely relabels the four g_i.

## Count words returning to one index

Write I for a nontrivial internal K4 step. Any two consecutive internal steps
can be replaced by either0 or1 internal step without altering the deck
element or the endpoints. Consecutive F,F⁻¹ steps cancel exactly. Thus every
walk of length≤3 can be compressed without increasing its length.

Fix a root index i and consider only targets of that same index(i,h'). For
each compressed word, its deck displacement is one fixed product of the
g_j and their inverses. Distinct words may coincide in H, so counting these
products bounds the number of different same-index targets for every H.

With zero external steps, the only displacement is the identity.

With one external step, its internal prefix/suffix can change indices at
most once each. The displacement is among

    g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3,g_0⁻¹,g_1⁻¹,g_2⁻¹,g_3⁻¹,

at most8 possibilities.

With two external steps, at most one internal step remains. Opposite external
directions either cancel or cannot return to index i if the internal step
is nontrivial. For two forward steps there are three possible placements:

- no middle/internal correction: product g_i*g_(P i), with index P²i;
- I,F,F: to end at i, the initial internal target is P⁻²i, giving
  g_(P⁻²i)*g_(P⁻¹i);
- F,I,F: to end at i, the middle internal target is P⁻¹i, giving
  g_i*g_(P⁻¹i).

The first product reaches same index either with no internal step when
P²i=i, or with a final internal step F,F,I when P²i≠i. The second and third
placements are legal exactly when P²i≠i. Reversing each route gives the inverse
product, with the order of factors reversed as required; no commutativity
is used.

Consequently, if i has cycle length1 or2 in P, two external steps contribute
at most the single pair(g_i*g_(P i))^{±1}, namely2 products. If its cycle
length is3 or4, they contribute at most3 inverse pairs, namely6 products.

With three external steps there is no internal step. Any direction change
cancels adjacent F,F⁻¹ and gives an already counted shorter word. Three
forward steps can return to index i only if P³i=i, adding at most the pair

    (g_i*g_(P i)*g_(P²i))^{±1}.

Thus a fixed index (cycle length1) has at most1+8+2+2=13 possible deck
displacements; cycle length2 has at most1+8+2=11; cycle length3 has at most
1+8+6+2=17; cycle length4 has at most1+8+6=15.

## Every permutation has a root with fewer than16 possibilities

| P cycle type | Same-index bounds for indices0,1,2,3 | A root bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| identity | 13,13,13,13 | 13 |
| (01) | 11,11,13,13 | 11 |
| (01)(23) | 11,11,11,11 | 11 |
| (012) | 17,17,17,13 | 13 at its fixed index |
| (0123) | 15,15,15,15 | 15 |

If |H|≥16, the16 or more vertices of the chosen same-index fiber cannot all
be reached within3. The graph therefore fails diameter3. More generally D3
requires |H| no greater than that chosen root bound, which is always≤15.

This also explains why adding K4 triangles did not cure the former controller
enlargement difficulty: the many internal triangle words all have identity
deck displacement. They do not create the missing same-index deck elements.

## Actual symbolic check and its scope

`symbolic_gate.py` enumerates all words of lengths0,1,2,3,156 per root, for
4 index roots and5 permutation types:3120 formal walks. It tracks both an
integer four-vector of exponents (the original abelian-voltage question) and
a reduced free-group word (the noncommutative question). Internal edges add
no symbol; F appends g_i and F⁻¹ appends g_(P⁻¹i)⁻¹, reducing adjacent inverse
letters only. Both forms give exactly the table above. All signatures and
shortest port-word representatives are saved in SYMBOLIC_CERTIFICATE.json.

For any actual voltage assignment or any homomorphic evaluation of these
free words in H, the number of resulting deck elements can only decrease.
This is why the finite symbolic certificate supports every assignment while
the actual numeric enumeration count remains0. It is not a claim to have
enumerated all groups, all5regular graphs or all possible fiber mechanisms.

The earlier whole-ball64slot bound and signed-voltage condition in PRED.json
were superseded before numerical execution; the same-index obstruction is
strictly enough. Stop this entire uniform K4/single-F organization at deck
order≥16. Do not start the retained C++ enumeration, another modulus, another
16-element deck group or a seed ladder. Existing C66 strict results and the
accepted C60/F4 graph are unchanged.
