This graph is constructed as a two-copy voltage lift over the semidirect product Z49 ⋉16 Z197, of order 9653. The group operation (following the notation of the original contributions to this table -see top of this page-) is defined by [x,y] × [u,v] =[(x+u) mod 49, (y·16u+v) mod 197]. The construction uses two copies of the group, labelled 0 and 1, with internal inverse-pair generators (48,0) and (39,177), respectively, corresponding to the integers 9456 and 7860. The copies are connected by two generators: (0,0) and (37,28), corresponding to the integers 0 and 7317, respectively. Each connecting generator also defines its inverse edge in the opposite direction. The resulting graph has 2·9653 = 19306 vertices, is 4-regular, and has diameter 10. The construction was obtained through an exhaustive symmetry-reduced search over two-vertex voltage lifts of odd-order metacyclic groups.
