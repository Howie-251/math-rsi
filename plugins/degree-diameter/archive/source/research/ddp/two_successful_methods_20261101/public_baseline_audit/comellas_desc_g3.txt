 This graph is the lift of a voltage graph with 2 vertices over the group SmallGroup(60,9) = C5 x A4 (so 2 x 60 = 120 vertices). Each of the two vertex types has one semi-edge (its voltage is an involution, giving a perfect matching inside the type) and five edges to the other type. Its automorphism group has order 60 with two orbits of 60 vertices, so it is not vertex--transitive. This construction is not derived from the 119-vertex graph (whose automorphism group has order 672 and three orbits).
It was found by a computer search over (number of base vertices) x (all groups of the given order, from GAP's SmallGroups library) x (orders just above the record), optimising the voltages by simulated annealing until every pair of vertices was within distance 3.
