Graph constructed as a regular voltage lift of a five-vertex base graph over the semidirect product Z8 ⋉4 Z5, of order 40. The group operation (following the notation of the original contributions to this table -see top of this page-) is defined by [a,b] × [u,v] = [(a+u) mod 8, (b·4u+v) mod 5]. In a voltage lift, each base vertex v is replaced by 40 vertices (v,g), one for each group element g in G. A directed base edge v → w with voltage t produces the edges (v,g)–(w,gt) for all g in G. The base graph has five vertices, labelled 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4, and five directed links: 0 → 1, 0 → 2, 0 → 3, and 1 → 4, all with identity voltage (0,0), and 1 → 2 with voltage (7,0). In addition, vertex 2 has a semi-edge with involution voltage (4,0), connecting (2,g) to (2,gt), and contributing one neighbor per lifted vertex. Vertex 3 has a loop with voltage (6,1), and vertex 4 has a loop with voltage (6,2); each loop contributes the two edges corresponding to its voltage and its inverse. The resulting graph has 5·40 = 200 vertices, 300 edges,is 3-regular, and has diameter 7. The construction was obtained through an exhaustive search of voltage assignments for five-vertex cubic base graphs over groups of order 40.

This graph is constructed as a regular voltage lift of a two-vertex base graph over the group G = SL(2,7) : C2, of order 672 (SmallGroup(672,1047) in GAP). In this voltage lift, each base vertex c is replaced by 672 vertices (c,g), one for each element g of G, and a directed edge from c to d with voltage W produces the edges (c,g)–(d,gW) for all g in G. The base graph has two vertices, labelled 0 and 1, each carrying two semi-edges, and the vertices are joined by two parallel edges. The semi-edges at vertex 0 have voltages W1 and W2, while those at vertex 1 have voltages W3 and W4. These four voltages are involutions, each contributing one neighbor to every lifted vertex. The two parallel edges from 0 to 1 have voltages W5 and W6, of orders 8 and 6, respectively, and their reverse edges carry the inverse voltages. The six voltages, given explicitly as permutations of 32 points, generate the entire group G. Each voltage below is a permutation of {1..32}, written as its 32 images:

This graph is constructed as a two-copy voltage lift over the non-metacyclic group G = ((F52 ⋊ C3) × Z5) ⋊ C8, of order 3000. The elements of F52 are represented by pairs (a,b), and the generator c of order 3 acts by the matrix B = [[0,4],[1,4]], while the generator y of order 8 acts by the matrix A = [[1,1],[2,4]] on F52, and sends c to c2 and z to z2, where z has order 5. The construction uses two copies of G, with inverse-pair generators y and ((0,2),c,z4)y4 for the first and second copies, respectively. The copies are connected by three generators: the identity 1, ((0,1),c,z)y4, and ((1,4),c,z4)y7. Each connecting generator also defines its inverse edge in the opposite direction. The resulting graph has 2·3000 = 6000 vertices, is 5-regular, and has diameter 7.
