# Complete scalar-affine F16 Petersen-cover NO: paper proof and actual rank binding

**Actual inputs.** The unique finite gate generated all nonbacktracking base words of length≤6 once. Every ordered nonadjacent Petersen slot has17 words; controller0 and controller2 are nonadjacent in the literal outer5-cycle/spoke model. `AFFINE_F16_RANK_RESULT.json` selects the actual17 saved word IDs for this one slot. For each of its17 possible omitted words it stores the15×6 integer differences of the other16 words from the first remaining reference. All17 matrices have reported column rank6 over both F3 and F5.

The independent paper review read `affine16_rank_once.py`: the routine reduces the integer rows modulo the selected prime, selects a nonzero pivot, swaps it into position, multiplies by its modular inverse and eliminates that pivot from every other row. Its rank count is the number of independent pivot columns. The primes3 and5 make every chosen nonzero pivot invertible. The loop dimensions are15×6. There is no field16 arithmetic in this calculation.

`PAPER_AFFINE16_BINDING_REVIEW.json` additionally binds all17 selected IDs and all1530 saved integer matrix entries to the existing word coefficient rows by literal integer equality. This small equality binding did not regenerate words or rerun a modular rank calculation. The reported rank values are read from the actual root computation, with its algorithm reviewed, rather than represented as an independently repeated numerical experiment.

**Proof.** Suppose a scalar-affine F16 cover of the fixed Petersen base had diameter≤6. Reverse edges use literal inverse affine bijections. Reduce immediate base backtracks, which lift to exact cancellations. For the base slot0→2, the complete17 reduced words therefore give17 affine maps f_i(x)=a_i x+b_i, and for every x their outputs cover all16 field elements.

At each x exactly one unordered pair has equal outputs. Summing over all16 x gives16 collisions. A pair of identical maps contributes16, a pair of distinct maps of equal slope contributes0, and a pair of different slopes contributes1. Thus16d+C=16, with d the number of identical pairs and C the number of different-slope pairs. If d=1, all17 slopes agree. If d=0, C=16; the only possible nonconstant slope multiplicity partition is16+1, since a largest class of size≤15 already forces at least30 different-slope pairs. In either case there is some omitted index k for which all16 remaining word slopes agree.

Gauge the nine spanning-tree edge affine maps to the identity by vertexwise affine coordinate changes. Six cotree affine maps remain. Write their nonzero slopes as powers of one generator of the cyclic group F16* of order15, with exponent vector s∈(Z/15)^6. Word slopes have exponents equal to their saved integer coefficient vectors dotted with s. Equality of the16 remaining slopes gives D_k s=0 modulo15 for one of the17 actual difference matrices.

That matrix has column rank6 modulo3 and modulo5. Reducing s first modulo3 and then modulo5 gives zero in both fields. By the Chinese remainder theorem s=0 modulo15. Therefore every cotree slope is1; tree slopes were already1. The normalized cover is translation-only on the additive group of F16, namely F2^4.

At a fixed base vertex the actual return-word count is25, including the empty word. The other24 nonempty closed nonbacktracking words split into twelve distinct inverse pairs. In an exponent-two translation group, a word and its inverse have identical voltage. Hence these25 words reach at most13 of the16 return-fiber vertices in≤6 steps. This contradicts diameter≤6.

**Conclusion.** No cover of this fixed Petersen base using one-dimensional scalar-affine F16 edge bijections and their literal inverses can have diameter≤6. This is a complete model-specific obstruction using one actual nonadjacent slot and all17 possible outlier deletions, not a sample or voltage-assignment search. It includes translation-only F2^4 as the final normalized subcase. It does not exclude arbitrary16-permutation covers, semilinear maps, higher-dimensional affine covers with different linear groups, another base, or general cubic diameter6 graphs. No graph, BFS, field table, voltage assignment, solver, Lean or external review was run by this paper review.
