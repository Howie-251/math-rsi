Michel_684

Degree= 7, Diameter = 4; Order =684; Moore bound=1814

Obtained by Michel de Messieres (michel@demessieres.com, October 5, 2026). The search programs were written and run by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under the author's direction. Lift of a voltage graph with two vertices over the semidirect product of cyclic groups Z9 (⋊23) Z38. Each vertex type has four neighbours of its own type and 3 of the other type. The automorphism group is the lifted group, with two orbits, so the graph is not vertex-transitive. They were found by a computer search over such groups of order just above half the former entry , optimising the voltages by simulated annealing until every pair of vertices was within distance 4.

Dhar_3444

Degree = 11, Diameter = 4; Order = 3444; Moore bound=12222.

Dharunish Yugeswardeenoo (dharyugi@gmail.com; Chalfont, PA, USA; October 7, 2026). Results obtained by using a hybrid discovery framework in development: LLM-guided search over classical algebraic constructions, combined with local search and exact completion. The specific LLMs used were OpenAI's Sol 5.6 and Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 Max.
Incidence graph of the symplectic generalized quadrangle W(9) (820 points, 820 lines, 10-regular, diameter 4, girth 8), doubled: every vertex gets two copies and a base edge a-b carrying a voltage x in {0,1} joins (a,i) to (b,i+x). The voltages are chosen (by local search) so that for each of the 597,780 pairs of opposite vertices the 10 geodesics between them do not all have the same voltage sum; both copies of the far vertex are then reached in 4 steps. The two copies of every line are joined to each other, which settles all pairs at base distance less than 4 and leaves each of the 1,640 point-copies one free slot. Those slots carry 164 twin vertices: for each of the 82 lines of a spread and each of the two sheets, a new vertex joined to the 10 point-copies adjacent to that copy of the line; the two twins of a spread line are adjacent. 3,280 + 164 = 3,444 vertices, 11-regular, diameter 4.

Com_13608

Degree = 15, Diameter = 4; Order =13608; Moore bound = 44326.

Obtained by Francesc Comellas (October 24, 2026), with the help of OpenAI GPT 6 Astra.
Graph constructed using a lift over F34 of a 168-vertex controller derived from the semidirect product G = Z6 ⋉11 Z56, of order 336. As in the original contributions from M.J. Dinneen and P. Hafner (see top of this page), the group operation is defined by [a,b] × [u,v] = [(a+u) mod 6, (b·11u+v) mod 56]. The controller is obtained using the group generators (5,0), (5,51), and (3,14), together with a polarity defined by the multiplier (3,0). Each controller vertex u is replaced by a fibre containing 34 = 81 vertices (u,p), where p belongs to F34. For each oriented controller edge u → w, the lifted edges are determined by affine maps over F3 of the form (u,p) ∼ (w,Akp + λbk), where λ ∈ F3, and Ak and bk are the specified 4×4 matrices and four-dimensional vectors associated with the three generator types. Additional edges within selected fibres complete the construction while keeping the maximum degree at 15. The resulting graph has 168·81 = 13608 vertices, 101493 edges, and diameter 4. It is not regular: 12474 vertices have degree 15 and the remaining 1134 vertices have degree 14. This construction improves the previous value of 13365 vertices by 243 vertices.
