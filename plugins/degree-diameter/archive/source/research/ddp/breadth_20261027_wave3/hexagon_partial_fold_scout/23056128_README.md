# Partial folds of the split Cayley hexagon H(9) and large graphs of diameter five

Author: Rishabh Rajiv (ORCID 0009-0000-8626-5234)
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23056128
License: CC BY 4.0

This deposit accompanies the paper of the same title. It gives three graphs that improve the known lower
bounds for the degree-diameter problem, the largest order N(d,k) of a graph of maximum degree at most d and
diameter at most k:

    (d,k)   new order    previous
    (11,5)       36563       35203
    (12,5)       41699       38167
    (13,5)       50750       44232

The previous bound is the largest published order of a graph of maximum degree at most d and diameter at
most k, as of September 2026: at (11,5) and (12,5) it is Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22907863, at (13,5) Zenodo
10.5281/zenodo.22928337.

The quotient F of the incidence graph of the split Cayley hexagon H(9) by a duality of order four is a
graph on 33397 vertices with maximum degree 10 and diameter 5 (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.22907863). It
identifies each point p of H(9) with the point tau^2 p. Partial folds keep some of these pairs apart,
joining the points through a pairing of points with lines built from the duality, and blow up the points of
the subhexagon H(3); twin vertices are added last.

## Files

- `partial-folds-split-cayley-hexagon-H9-large-graphs-diameter-five.pdf`: the paper.
- `data-and-verification.zip`, which contains:
  - `graph_d11k5_n36563.edges.xz`, `graph_d12k5_n41699.edges.xz` and `graph_d13k5_n50750.edges.xz`:
    the three graphs, xz-compressed; a line "n m", then one edge "u v" per line, vertices numbered from 0.
  - `witnesses/pf_*.json`: for each graph, the split fibers and their colours (bit strings over the
    generic fibers), the copy counts of the 364 points of H(3), the vertices copied by the further twins
    and the SHA-256 hash of the edge list (of the uncompressed file).
  - `tau.txt.xz`: the duality, as a permutation of the 132860 vertices of the incidence graph of
    H(9).
  - `verify_witness.py`: a standalone verifier (Python 3, standard library only).
  - `allsrc.c`: an independent all-sources breadth-first search in C with OpenMP (usage in its header).

## Verification

`python3 verify_witness.py` checks every graph (its pinned hash, simple, the stated maximum degree,
eccentricity 5 from a fixed sample of sources) and prints `ALL CHECKS PASS`. With `--full` it runs
breadth-first search from every vertex. With `--geometry` it rebuilds H(9) from its coordinates,
checks that tau is a duality of order four whose square fixes 728 vertices (the subhexagon H(3)),
and rebuilds every graph from its witness and the geometry and compares it with the shipped edge
list (a few minutes). The intermediate graphs listed in Appendix B of the paper are not included.
