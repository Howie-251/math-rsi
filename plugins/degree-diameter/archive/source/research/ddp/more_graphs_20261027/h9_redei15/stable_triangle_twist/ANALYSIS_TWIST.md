# A second stable real form of the same Rédei blocker

This is a valid new Galois form of the existing fifteen-point blocking set, with an orbit disjoint from the old 702 masks. It changes the actual mandatory neighborhoods while retaining the core-coverage theorem. It does not imply coverage of the current extra vertices. The completed degree pool and allowed-anchor tests found no extension in this frozen new orbit.

Use F9=F3[u], u^2=2, with u encoded by3. Its nonzero squares form mu4={1,2,3,6}. Let B+++ contain the three coordinate vertices and the four points e_i+t e_j on each coordinate side, for i<j and t in mu4. This has fifteen points. Every line with a zero coefficient contains a coordinate vertex. If all three coefficients are nonzero and none of its three side intersections belonged to B+++, all three corresponding coefficient ratios would be nonsquares. The product of two is the third, a contradiction. Here minus one is a square. Hence B+++ blocks all lines. A vertex has a tangent by choosing a nonsquare ratio on the opposite side; a side-interior point has a tangent by choosing the remaining coefficient so that both other side ratios are nonsquares. Thus the blocker is minimal.

The three coordinate sides are exactly its six-secants: a different line meets their union in at most three distinct points. Any projective stabilizer must therefore permute this triangle. Its diagonal ratios must belong to mu4, so its full projective stabilizer is mu4^2 semidirect S3, of order96. This is the all-square triangle model of the previously used projective Rédei shape; it is not a new blocking-set size or a claimed classification of all real forms.

Set R=[[1,0,0],[0,1,1],[0,u,-u]] and Btw=R B+++. Frobenius phi:x->x^3 satisfies phi(R)=R P12, where P12 interchanges the last two coordinates and preserves B+++. Consequently Btw is phi-stable. In the original triangle coordinates its descent involution is P12 phi. It fixes only e0 among the three triangle vertices. It fixes all four interior points of the side joining e1,e2, since their ratios t transform to t^(-3)=t. The other eight side-interior points form four conjugate pairs. Thus Btw has five fixed points and five conjugate pairs. By contrast, B+++ itself has nine fixed points and three pairs; it was retained only as a comparison, not pooled in this task.

The old 702 real form has all three intersections of its three six-secants fixed by phi. Btw has only one fixed triangle vertex. Any PGL3(F3) map commutes with phi and must carry the uniquely determined six-secant triangle to the other triangle. The two rational orbits are therefore disjoint, even though both have five fixed points and five pairs. The rational stabilizer of Btw has order8: its permutation part centralizes (12), giving two choices, and after diagonal normalization its entries satisfy d2=d1^(-1), d1 in mu4, giving four choices. Since |PGL3(F3)|=5616, the orbit has size702. These are paper counts, independently confirmed by the scout's single tiny computation.
