# Current cubic diameter-10 source: Conder C1250.2

The [current Comellas diameter-10 description](https://web.mat.upc.edu/francesc.comellas/delta-d/desc_g/desc_g10.html) links the literal `C1250.2.graph.txt`. This is Conder's source, communicated in 2006, rather than an Exoo recipe. Its author census identifies C1250.2 as non-bipartite, of girth 16, with a 3-arc-regular automorphism group of order 15000. Hence it has one vertex orbit and one undirected edge orbit. The [official Sage script](https://web.mat.upc.edu/francesc.comellas/delta-d/desc_g/adjacencies/adjD10/1250_Conder_SageMath.txt) also reports vertex/edge transitivity and that it is non-Cayley. These symmetry and diameter facts are inherited; no automorphism search or source diameter BFS was run.

The downloaded [official raw literal](https://web.mat.upc.edu/francesc.comellas/delta-d/desc_g/adjacencies/adjD10/C1250.2.graph.txt) has labels 1 through 1250. Direct row checks give 1250 vertices, 1875 edges, no loops or duplicates, reciprocal adjacency, and degree exactly 3 everywhere. Union-find proves connectedness, and parity union-find confirms non-bipartiteness without a graph-distance search. `source1250.edges` is precisely that literal with every label reduced by one; its header is `1250 1875` and the first edge is `(0,1)`. `SOURCE_PROFILE.json` records the checks and the exact normalization. The separately downloaded official Sage sparse6 representation has the same order/size but a different vertex labeling. No Sage-to-raw isomorphism was computed or assumed.

The [author's census](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~conder/symmcubic2048list.txt) gives the identifier and symmetry type. Its [type-3 relations file](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~conder/symmcubicgraphrelations-type3.txt), saved locally, has the specific C1250.2 presentation on generators a,h,p,q at lines 222–227. The cited Conder–Ma paper, Theorem 7.1(c) and Remark 7.2, describes the 1250 example as an abelian regular cover of the Petersen graph with deck group C5^3. No corresponding 10-by-125 coordinate map into the current raw labels has been recovered here; it must not be guessed from factorization.

A targeted ledger lookup found the cubic200 single-edge subdivisions and AFY70 ordinary covers, but no exact C1250.2 operation already closed. Those earlier failures are different sources.

The concrete route opportunity is a single anchored edge subdivision: edge transitivity reduces all 1875 possible source edges to the representative `(0,1)`. Its new root is within 10 of every old target exactly when `R9(0) union R9(1)` is the entire source. Non-bipartiteness makes this a genuine question: a target can be at distance 10 from both adjacent endpoints. A successful new-root gate still leaves old-old pairs to check, because the old edge has been replaced by a longer path. No such distance test or candidate was executed in this source-recovery task.
