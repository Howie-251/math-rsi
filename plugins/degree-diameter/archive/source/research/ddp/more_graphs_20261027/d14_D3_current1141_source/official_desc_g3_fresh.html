Michel_120

Degree = 6, Diameter = 3; Order =120; Moore bound=187;

Obtained by Michel de Messieres (michel@demessieres.com, October 4, 2026). The search programs were written and run by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) under the author's direction. This graph is the lift of a voltage graph with 2 vertices over the group SmallGroup(60,9) = C5 x A4 (so 2 x 60 = 120 vertices). Each of the two vertex types has one semi-edge (its voltage is an involution, giving a perfect matching inside the type) and five edges to the other type. Its automorphism group has order 60 with two orbits of 60 vertices, so it is not vertex--transitive. This construction is not derived from the 119-vertex graph (whose automorphism group has order 672 and three orbits). It was found by a computer search over (number of base vertices) x (all groups of the given order, from GAP's SmallGroups library) x (orders just above the record), optimising the voltages by simulated annealing until every pair of vertices was within distance 3.
The graph and a detailed write-up are also on Zenodo https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23136968

M. Conder found (2011) an optimal Cayley graph (7,3)=168 (non isomorphic with the Exoo graph, which is not Cayley). Clik here for more details and the edge list (original information from Marston Conder at combinatoricswiki.org).
F. Comellas (May 2026) found four other non-isomorphic Cayley graphs with properties different from those of Conder's graph. They arise from the group SmallGroup(168,43) and are denoted SG168x43x1, SG168x43x2, SG168x43x4, and SG168x43x5. The graphs SG168x43x1 and SG168x43x2 have girth 4 and an automorphism group of order 336, as does Conder's graph. In contrast, SG168x43x4 has girth 3 and an automorphism group of order 168, while SG168x43x5 has girth 5 and also an automorphism group of order 168. The numbers of cycles and the values of the algebraic connectivity are also different.

Start: the 585-vertex graph obtained by folding the generalized quadrangle over GF(8) by its polarity (the (9,3) construction of the Q'8 family); its 65 absolute ("self-friendly") vertices are those on their own polar line. - "Untied twins": for each of the 65 absolute vertices g we added 4 twins, each adjacent to the 8 other vertices of g's line but not to g (845 vertices; this frees many degree slots while keeping diameter 3). - A layer of 22 new vertices whose edges, together with extra edges in the unused slots of the existing vertices, were placed by targeted simulated annealing until the diameter was 3: order 867, maximum degree 13 (only 1 unused slot). - Order 868: starting from the 867-vertex graph, one more layer vertex was added and the annealed edges (layer edges and the extra edges placed in unused slots) were re-optimised; about 890 of the 5,635 edges were moved, the rest kept. A simple 15-regular graph on V(C) × F33, where C s a 54-vertex simple 5-regular Cayley graph. The route-chart lift construction of [arXiv:2606.15860] is generalized to allow a self-inverse label and transition matrices depending on directed controller edges.
