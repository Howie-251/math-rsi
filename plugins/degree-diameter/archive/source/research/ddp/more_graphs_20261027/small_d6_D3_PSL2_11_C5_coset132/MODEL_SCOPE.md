# Fixed PSL2(F11) coset-action model and complete finite scope

The group is G=SL2(F11)/{plusminus I}. A matrix(a,b,c,d) is represented by the lexicographically smaller of itself and its negative modulo11; multiplication is matrix multiplication modulo11 followed by this canonicalization. Actual enumeration gives660 projective determinant-one matrices. INPUT_TABLES.json and group_table.u16le.bin bind every group product and inverse to this representation, rather than assuming a group label.

H is generated by diag(3,4), of actual order5. Vertices are the132 cosets xH; vertex0 is H. Let t=(0,1;10,0). Its projective square is identity and it normalizes H, giving the matching doublecoset HtH of size5. For an involution g outside N, the intersection H and gHg^-1 is trivial: a nontrivial intersection of two groups of prime order5 would make them equal and put g in N. Thus HgH has25 elements and gives five neighbors per vertex. It is inverse-closed because g is an involution. HtH and HgH are disjoint from each other and from H. Their union D defines adjacency

## Why exactly10 valency5 choices, and why this covers the fixed action

There are55 projective nonidentity involutions, of which5 are in N and50 outside. In an outside doublecoset HgH, an element h1*g*h2 is an involution exactly when, after conjugation by h1, g*u is an involution for u=h2*h1 in H. This would require g*u*g=u^-1. A nonidentity u generates H and would put g in N, which is excluded. Hence u must be identity and this gives exactly5 involutions in that doublecoset. The50 outside involutions therefore partition into10 distinct inverse-closed size25 doublecosets.

Conversely, inversion is an involution on any inverse-closed size25 doublecoset. Odd cardinality forces a fixed point, which is an involution because the doublecoset is disjoint from identity. Thus this involution enumeration finds every self-paired valency5 suborbit.

The actual complete doublecoset enumeration has28 parts: two of size5(the loop coset H and matching coset HtH), plus26 of size25. Among the26 outside suborbits,10 are self-paired and16 form inverse-paired pairs; an undirected graph using such a nonselfpaired pair already has degree10. Two valency5 suborbits also exceed degree6. A graph using only one valency5 orbital or a lower-degree subset cannot have diameter3 at132 vertices: the degree5 Moore bound is106. It follows that every simple undirected G-invariant graph of maximum degree at most6 in this fixed action that could have diameter3 must be exactly one of the10 valency5-plus-matching unions tested here.

This excludes the fixed action family, not all132-vertex graphs, all possible vertex-transitive graphs, every PSL2 construction, or the general(6,3) parameter. The earlier660-vertex norm5 arithmetic(6,4) experiment has a different vertex set and connection rule and was not repeated. This construction allows a nonregular G action; no claim is made that a candidate graph could not have a separate regular automorphism group.
