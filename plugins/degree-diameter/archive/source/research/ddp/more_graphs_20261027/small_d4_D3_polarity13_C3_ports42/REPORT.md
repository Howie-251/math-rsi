# Polarity13 C3-port42 template: prepared then strictly excluded without SAT

The fixedP3 prediction is refuted by a uniform route obstruction. The actual base has four absolute, six secant and three external points. Every absolute port reaches at most three of the nine external lifts in two old steps. A helper needs at least three direct spokes to reach all nine in three steps, but the middle of P3 has only two. It must miss at least three external targets for every voltage and port assignment. No solver was called and no chosen42-point graph was generated.

The stronger three-helper completion family is also excluded. Every helper would need internal helper degree at most1. Any length<=3 route joining different helpers through old vertices violates a port's single added-edge slot, or requires an old absolute-absolute edge. Thus the internal helper graph must be connected, which is impossible on three vertices with maximumdegree1. This does not claim that the entire expanded graph is disconnected; its relevant helper pair can simply be too far apart.
