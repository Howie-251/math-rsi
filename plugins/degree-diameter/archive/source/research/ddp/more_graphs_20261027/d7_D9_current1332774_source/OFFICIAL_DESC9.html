The group operation (following the notation of the original contributions to this table -see top of this page-) is defined by [x,y] × [u,v] = [(x+u) mod 36, (y·18u+v) mod 73]. The construction uses three copies of the group, labelled 0, 1, and 2. Copies 0 and 1 are connected by three generators (0,0), (26,50), and (22,7), corresponding to the integers 0, 1948, and 1613, respectively. Copies 0 and 2 are connected by the identity generator (0,0), while copies 1 and 2 are connected by the generator (30,8), corresponding to the integer 2198. Copy 2 has an internal inverse-pair generator (33,0), corresponding to the integer 2409. Each connecting generator also defines its inverse edge in the opposite direction. The resulting graph has 3·2628 = 7884 vertices, is 4-regular, and has diameter 9.
