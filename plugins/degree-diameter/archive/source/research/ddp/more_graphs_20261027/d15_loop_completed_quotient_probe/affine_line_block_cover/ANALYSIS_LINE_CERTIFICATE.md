# Affine three-pin lines: sufficient block certificate

The proposed certificate is valid and may break inversion. It is a sufficient subproblem of the same single-new-point extension question, not a new graph mechanism. This review performs no mask calculation, solver, BFS or new ball computation.

Each certified free space F_s has dimension3 over F3. It has13 projective directions; each direction has9 affine cosets, giving117 distinct three-point lines per controller. The two sources therefore have2808 and3510 raw line columns. Every member is an actual free old vertex. A line not through zero is generally distinct from its inverse, so selecting it does not impose the previously tested inversion constraint.

For a line L, let C(L) be the OR of the already cached radius-(D-1) balls of its three pins. Its block mask contains controller j exactly when all q vertices of that target fiber lie in C(L). If at most five selected line masks cover every target controller, every old target belongs to the covered full fiber of at least one selected line. The expanded old-pin set S is the union of the selected lines, so |S|<=3*5=15. Shared pins are deduplicated and receive only one new spoke. All pins are free, giving a legal single append; complete old-target coverage supplies the new-root diameter bound while all old edges remain.

Identical block masks may keep only the first literal line witness. Replacing a selected line by that witness preserves the block-cover certificate and the at-most-five line count; even if overlap with other lines changes, the bound |S|<=15 still holds. Empty block masks may be discarded. This reduction does not claim equality of the entire ball unions or preserve incomplete-block gains, which are not used by this certificate.

The completeness boundary is narrow. A failure only rejects full-controller certificates in which each controller is covered completely by at least one selected line. Several lines might jointly cover a target fiber even though no individual line covers it completely; such a genuine at-most-five-line full cover is outside this model. Configurations using more than five overlapping lines with at most fifteen distinct pins, and arbitrary free-pin sets, are also outside it. The actual full independent model is a separate parallel test.

The use of actual ball unions is essential for offsets to matter. Along a controller word, line p+T reaches M*p+M*T+W. A single-word full-rank condition M*T+W=V is independent of p and could be replaced by the through-zero line T. In contrast, several proper affine cosets from different words may jointly fill a fiber only after the offset shifts them. For example, three words with common codimension-one W, all mapping T into W, but mapping p to the three distinct classes of V/W, cover V from p+T while their through-zero images coincide in W. This is a mathematical possibility, not an asserted source calculation. The producer therefore must use the complete cached union, as specified.

For any SAT line selection, expand literal witnesses to distinct original labels and check their actual free status, cardinality and the complete original-n cached OR before reporting a candidate. A block-level assignment without this expansion is not a graph. A negative or unknown solver result retains the restricted certificate scope above. Exact selected-line count, retained first witnesses and any budget cursor belong to the producer's receipts; this paper invents none.
