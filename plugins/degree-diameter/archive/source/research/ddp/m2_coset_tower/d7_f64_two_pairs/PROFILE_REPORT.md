## Actual finite input and lift scope

The field remains `F2[t]/(t^6+t+1)`, with r=t^3 of order 21. The original
group is `F64 semidirect C42`; the central involution k=(0,21) fixes all
block and head vertices. Its orbit quotient has group `F64 semidirect C21`,
Hbar generated by (0,7), and Kbar=C21. There are **1,344 quotient leaves,
448 blocks and 64 heads**, totaling 1,856 vertices. Quotient loops and
parallel edges may be discarded for distance calculations.

The two projected involution components are `(1,0)` and `(y,0)` for all
64 field elements y. Identity and repeated projections are retained.
The two projected noninvolutory inverse pairs are an unordered multiset
drawn from all **640** quotient inverse pairs. The complete raw range is
therefore **64*C(641,2)=13,127,680**. The independently derived number of
HH-passing inputs is **5,693,310**; this full count was not recomputed by
the prefix profile.

`profile.quotient_pairs.json` stores the actual 640 representatives in
increasing encoded group-id order, where `id=x+64*j` and the representative
is less than its inverse. Enumeration is lexicographic in y, a, b with
`0<=a<=b<640`. `profile_metadata.json` records the field, normalization,
subgroups, labels, complete input range, order and lift rules.

The only normalization used here is simultaneous nonzero field scaling
to fix one nonzero involution projection at 1; it preserves H and K.
There must be a nonzero involution projection because k is the sole
nonidentity involution above zero, and the two actual involutions are
distinct. No further projective or quotient-unit reduction was imposed.

Each projected noninvolutory inverse pair has exactly two distinct C42
inverse-pair lifts. Repeated projected pairs require those two different
lifts. A nonzero involution projection x has lifts `(x,0)` and `(x,21)`;
zero has only the legal nonidentity lift k. When y=1, the two involution
lifts must be different. Consequently every raw projected input admits
at least one legal original generator set. These facts preserve the
entire two-pair/two-involution branch, including repeated or identity
projections; they do not make every lift diameter 5.

## Exact filtering and calibration

The first filter checks HH by its actual 64-bit multiplicative-orbit
coverage mask. The next computes the exact block-to-block set

`Hbar W3 Hbar union Kbar W1 Hbar union Hbar W1 Kbar`,

using seven field-coordinate masks, one for each block index modulo 7.
For a word `(x,j)`, left Hbar multiplication replaces x by its three
`<r^7>` multiples and leaves j modulo 7 unchanged. Right Hbar multiplication
does not change the block. Both terms passing through a head are retained.
All words of lengths zero through three are included, even when projected
generator components coincide or are the identity.

Only a BB pass triggers ordinary depth-five BFS from the quotient block
and head roots, checking **all three target layers**. Because k fixes
those roots, their distances to both original leaves in each central
orbit coincide with the quotient distance. This makes those two quotient
root tests exact for the original graph. Quotient leaf-to-leaf coverage
does **not** certify original leaf-to-leaf coverage. A later lift backend
must check the latter separately.

The source agent independently projected the already saved three-pair
best graph and checked the central action and fixed-root distances in
`CENTRAL_QUOTIENT_CHECK.json`. This frontend was then compared with that
baseline: all **448 individual BB targets** and the missing counts for
all three target layers at both roots agree. The quotient block-root
misses are `(15,42,6)` and head-root misses `(0,42,0)`.
`preflight.json` and `baseline.json` record this calibration. The original
three-pair candidate enumeration was not repeated.

The best prefix representative, cursor 3,979, has y=0 and pair indices

## Cost estimate and remaining decision

The measured HH-plus-BB frontend cost projects to **about 15.0 CPU seconds**
for all 13,127,680 raw inputs and 5,693,310 HH passes. The estimate scales
the measured BB time by the known full HH count and the remaining timing
and loop overhead by the full raw count. The profile is short, so this is
a throughput estimate, not a precise runtime guarantee.

No BB pass occurred in the prefix, hence there is no measured survivor
rate or observed downstream cost. In particular, the 15.0-second estimate
**excludes unobserved block/head BFS and C42 lift costs**, and zero prefix
passes cannot be extrapolated as zero full-range passes. The full branch
remains unenumerated pending the parent's production-budget decision.
Sources and exact cursor are saved; no other parameter or generator
composition was started.
