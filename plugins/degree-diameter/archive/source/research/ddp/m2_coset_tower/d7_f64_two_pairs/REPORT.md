# F64 two-pair/two-involution branch: complete BB exclusion

The entire normalized **13,127,680-input** range is complete. Exactly
**5,693,310 inputs passed HH**, agreeing with the independent mathematical
count; **none passed the exact block-to-block five-step condition**.
Every HH-passing input misses at least **18 of the 448 blocks** from the
block root. The terminal status is `COMPLETE_NO_BH_CANDIDATES`.

Because the central quotient preserves distances whenever a block or
head is an endpoint, this finite check excludes diameter 5 for the
specified **F64 semidirect C42 tower with two noninvolutory inverse pairs
and two involutions**. All legal central lifts are covered by this
necessary-condition failure. No lift enumeration was needed or run.
This is a computational exclusion of this exact construction branch,
not a general bound for degree 7 and diameter 5, and not a new graph
record or Lean certification.

## Why this covers the declared branch

The field is `F2[t]/(t^6+t+1)` and r=t^3 has order 21. The central
involution k=(0,21) fixes every block and head of the original 3,200-point
tower. Its orbit quotient has 1,344 leaves, 448 blocks and 64 heads,
with group `F64 semidirect C21`, Hbar generated by (0,7), and Kbar=C21.
The original quotient order 42 is retained in the lift interpretation.

Simultaneous field scaling fixes one nonzero involution projection at
`(1,0)`. The second is `(y,0)` for all y in F64, including y=0 and y=1.
The projected noninvolutory inverse pairs form an unordered multiset
of two of the 640 available quotient pairs, including a repeated pair.
This gives exactly `64*C(641,2)` inputs. No further projective or
quotient-unit reduction was used. The actual representative list and
enumeration order are saved in `frontend.quotient_pairs.json` and
`profile_metadata.json`.

Every quotient pair has two different original C42 inverse-pair lifts.
A repeated projected pair must use both. Nonzero involution projections
have two lifts; the zero projection has only k as a legal nonidentity
lift, and y=1 requires the two involutions to have different lifts.
Thus every original generator set in this branch is represented by the
enumerated projections. [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) gives the independent
normalization and count proof; [LIFT_ROUTE_CRITERIA.md](LIFT_ROUTE_CRITERIA.md)
describes the exact lift criterion that was not needed here.

The BB front end computes

`Hbar W3 Hbar union Kbar W1 Hbar union Hbar W1 Kbar`

as seven 64-bit masks, retaining both head-mediated terms and all words
of lengths zero through three. Repeated or identity projected components
are not removed from the declared input. The set formula is exact by
[FULL_ROUTE_CRITERIA.md](../FULL_ROUTE_CRITERIA.md). Since the central
action fixes every block, a quotient BB failure is an actual original
BB failure for every central lift. There is no assumption that quotient
leaf-to-leaf distances are sufficient.
