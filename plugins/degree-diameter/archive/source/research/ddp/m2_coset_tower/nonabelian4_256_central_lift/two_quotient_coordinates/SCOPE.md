# Exactly two changed quotient coordinates

This run uses the original independently checked Q64 source in ../../nonabelian4_256_search/best.json, whose radius-three defect was 832 bad pairs. It does not use the one-coordinate run's best graph. Exactly two distinct members of the 13 free quotient voltages are changed, and both are required to differ from their original value. Thus the precise scope has C(13,2)×31² = 74,958 projections, disjoint from both the original projection and all 403 one-coordinate projections.

The Q64 word, central-bit, GF2 affine-system, legality, and exact sparse scoring core is copied unchanged from the completed parent-directory implementation. Each selected quotient permits all 13 center bits to vary jointly. Parallel projected voltage repetitions remain legal when their actual center bits make the edges distinct. No x normalization, seed queue, or wider coordinate neighborhood is introduced.

The first filter rejects an empty quotient target slot. For quotient-complete projections, the legal-center affine system is checked. A new necessary filter then counts slots having only one distinct affine form (mask,b); the path length is ignored for this distinctness test. Such a slot reaches only one lift for every center assignment, so a positive count rigorously rules out diameter three without enumerating center masks. The per-projection count and its overall minimum are saved. This is precisely the constant bad-slot obstruction observed in all eight previously scored one-coordinate projections.

Only projections passing these filters enter exact scoring of all 8,192 center masks, with illegal centers separately counted. No joint-condition filter is added. The first actual entered projection and its first legal center mask receive the single independent four-root calibration using the existing parent-directory calibrator. If none enters, there is no central assignment, graph, or BFS calibration to run. All projections retain their exact terminal category in projections.jsonl.

The fixed traversal is lexicographic in the two free-edge indices, then in their two alternative quotient codes. Stop on D3 or at the single 180-CPU-second budget including preparation, compilation, and calibration; no automatic restart is permitted. Any saved best is only best among the legal center assignments actually scored, because necessarily rejected projections are not fully scored. The parent owns the final saved-best all-256-root verification.
