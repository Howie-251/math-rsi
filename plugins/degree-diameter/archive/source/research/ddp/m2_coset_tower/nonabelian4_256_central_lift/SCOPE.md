# One-edge quotient changes with all joint central lifts

The authorized source is the independently checked Q64 graph in ../nonabelian4_256_search/best.json. Its quotient voltages are encoded a+16s in D32. Exactly one of the 13 free quotient voltages is changed to each of its 31 other values, giving 403 projections in fixed edge/value order. The source projection itself is excluded. The three tree voltages remain identity.

For a fixed projection, each free actual Q64 voltage is (a+16z_e,s), with 13 jointly variable center bits. All 16 base edge IDs are retained, even when their projections coincide. Parallel projected edges can coincide twice if their center bits differ; three equal projected voltages cannot have a simple lift. No extra normalization or random start is used.

For each of four roots the code enumerates the 457 nonbacktracking base walks of length at most three. Only the true reversed dart of the same edge is forbidden. The zero-bit walk value is computed with actual Q64 multiplication and inverse, including the odd inverse's extra 16. Every walk's center bit is an affine form b XOR parity(mask & z). These walks are grouped into 4×128 quotient target slots.

An empty slot misses both lifts for every z and rules out diameter three for that projection. Such a projection is rejected before central scoring and no graph or BFS is generated for it. For a nonempty slot, all path forms have the same value exactly when their differences with one chosen form vanish. The resulting GF2 affine system defines the bad-center subspace: it adds one to the radius-three deficit precisely on its solution set. An inconsistent system adds nothing; a rank-zero system contributes a constant one. Sparse enumeration of each consistent solution subspace produces exact scores for all 8192 center masks. Illegal parallel duplicates are then excluded. No further necessary-only filter is used for projections entering central scoring.

The implementation records subspace ranks and the number of sparse score increments, alongside the naive 512×8192 slot-by-mask operation count. This measures the work avoided by the selected sparse implementation; it does not claim a measured runtime for an unimplemented bitset alternative.

The sum of four root deficits is exact, and left Q64 translation gives 32 times that sum as bad unordered pairs. Production performs no BFS. The first formal projection entering central scoring, at its first legal center mask, retains every path form and its actual reused score. Independent Python constructs that one actual graph and runs four full BFS roots, comparing all 1024 root-target radius-three distances pointwise. If no projection enters the center stage, this calibration and its BFS count are both zero.

The best saved graph is the minimum score among legal central lifts of quotient-complete projections actually scored. Since quotient-rejected projections are not centrally scored, this is not a claim of global minimum deficit over all 403 projections. Completion with no D3 nonetheless excludes D3 throughout the specified 403-projection lift range. It says nothing about changing two quotient variables or all Q64 voltage graphs. The parent alone performs the final saved-best all-256-root check. The one-worker CPU budget is 180 seconds including preparation, compilation, and the sole formal calibration; no restart or extension is authorized.
