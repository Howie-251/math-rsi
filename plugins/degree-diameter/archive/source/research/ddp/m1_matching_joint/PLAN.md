# Joint matching profile: old paths and new roots together

The ten prior complete 2812-vertex candidates failed mainly on old–old pairs after deletion. The best had 350 such failures and 14 new–old failures. This profile changes the actual matching-selection objective to include mandatory old path damage; it is not another coverage-only seed.

For each old edge e, precompute C_e consisting of all unordered pairs that become farther than five when e alone is deleted. The original distance-5-only lists are kept for comparison. The stronger lists use the common-edge intersection of every walk of length at most five and also cover damaged pairs originally closer than five. Independent single-edge all-root BFS checks seven edge-orbit representatives. The original-graph distance matrix is reused.

For six selected disjoint oriented edges, take the set union of their C_e. Among this union count those pairs that cannot satisfy any of the four new-vertex repair inequalities even using optimistic endpoint distances in the original graph. Add the two new-root missing counts under the same optimistic distances. This is a proved lower bound on actual bad unordered pairs. A positive score proves failure; zero only triggers full graph verification. The score may omit jointly damaged pairs and endpoint distances lengthened by deleting several edges.

Before the profile, compare all ten old candidates against independent Python scores. This is a direct regression of the new objective, not a repeat of graph validation. A weaker distance-5-only C++ regression has already matched all ten independently computed scores.

One new profile uses one CPU, seed 20261027, at most 120 CPU seconds including calibration, and no additional group or source graph. It starts at the best of the ten existing candidates under the new objective. Six predetermined annealing segments start from the saved best except the fourth, which starts from the old mixed-coverage candidate 07. Initial temperature is calibrated from at most 256 legal proposal deltas, with a median uphill acceptance probability of one fifth. Temperature falls by a factor of 50 during each segment.

Proposals mix orientation flips, replacement by an arbitrary edge, changes preserving one endpoint, a single/double replacement directed at a current mandatory bad pair, and two-edge changes. A directed single replacement uses the exact original-distance endpoint-ball condition derived in `REPAIR_GEOMETRY.md` for pairs originally at distance at least four. This guarantees repair only of the selected pair in the optimistic relaxation, not of the actual complete graph. Every proposed matching must still have twelve distinct endpoints.

Stop on the first zero lower bound, budget exhaustion, or termination signal. Save best and final full edge lists, oriented matching parameters, score decomposition, calibration and actual terminal status. Independently rebuild and check both complete graphs after termination. If a zero-score graph fails, identify the omitted paths before any further search. No automatic continuation or extra seed follows a failure. This finite heuristic profile cannot exclude the matching-augmentation family.
