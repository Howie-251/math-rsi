# Large graphs of diameter five from a duality of order four of the split Cayley hexagon H(9)

Author: Rishabh Rajiv (ORCID 0009-0000-8626-5234)
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22907863
License: CC BY 4.0

This deposit accompanies the paper of the same title. The quotient of the incidence graph of the
split Cayley hexagon H(9) by a duality of order four is a graph F on 33397 vertices with maximum
degree 10 and diameter 5. Completed to a 10-regular graph and extended by twin vertices, it gives
new lower bounds for the degree-diameter problem in three cases:

    (d,k)   new order    previous
    (10,5)       33397       14047
    (11,5)       35203       20952
    (12,5)       38167       34992

The previous bound is the largest published order of a graph of maximum degree at most d and
diameter k, as of September 2026.

## Files

- `large-graphs-diameter-five-duality-order-four-split-cayley-hexagon-H9.pdf`: the paper.
- `data-and-verification.zip`, which contains:
  - `graph_d10k5_n33397.edges.xz`: the quotient F.
  - `graph_d10k5_n33397_regular.edges.xz`: the 10-regular completion of F, for (10,5).
  - `graph_d11k5_n35203.edges.xz` and `graph_d12k5_n38167.edges.xz`: the completion with twin
    vertices added, for (11,5) and (12,5).
  - `tau.txt.xz`: the duality, as a permutation of the 132860 vertices of the incidence graph of
    H(9).
  - `verify_witness.py`: a standalone verifier (Python 3, standard library only).
  - `allsrc.c`: an independent all-sources breadth-first search in C (usage in its header).

Each edge list is a header line "n m" followed by one edge "u v" per line, with vertices numbered
from 0. The SHA-256 hash of every uncompressed file is pinned in the verifier.

## Verification

`python3 verify_witness.py` checks every graph (simple, the stated maximum degree, eccentricity 5
from a fixed sample of sources) and the twin structure of the last two graphs, and prints
`ALL CHECKS PASS`. With `--full` it runs breadth-first search from every vertex. With `--geometry` it
rebuilds H(9) from its coordinates, checks that tau is a duality of order four whose square fixes
728 vertices, and reconstructs F edge by edge.
