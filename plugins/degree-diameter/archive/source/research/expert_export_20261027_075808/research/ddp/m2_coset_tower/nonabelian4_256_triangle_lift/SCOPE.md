# Prescribed central triangles: bounded construction profile

The underlying graph has four Q64 orbits of size64 and K4 edge multiplicities `[3,3,2,2,3,3]`, giving 256 vertices and degree8 when the parallel-edge voltages are distinct. The target is diameter3. This profile restricts the voltage family by forcing two specified triangles to the central element c=(16,0). It does not enumerate the entire restricted family.

Tree edges0,3,6 remain identity. The eleven independent edges, in center-bit order, are `[1,2,4,5,7,8,9,10,11,13,14]`. Full Q64 voltages on the dependent edges are `h12=inv(h2)*c*h7` and `h15=inv(h9)*c*h11`. These equations guarantee that all four orbit roots reach their central mates within three steps. Their candidate-preserving parameterization and exact coverage proof are in `TRIANGLE_LIFT_MATH.md`.

For each D32 projection, independent-edge baselines use the canonical section; dependent baselines are computed by the displayed full Q64 equations and retain their high bits. Their center coefficient masks are respectively18 and320 in the eleven-bit order. Equal projected parallel edges impose `(mask_e XOR mask_f).z = 1 XOR baselinebit_e XOR baselinebit_f`. The full GF2 system must be consistent. Neither a simple multiplicity-only shortcut nor the old independent13-bit masks are used.

All freely reduced base walks of length at most3 are evaluated:457 per root, grouped into512 quotient target slots across four roots. Exact duplicate affine central forms are removed, preserving the shortest length. With Nempty empty slots and Nsingleton slots having one distinct form, `L=2*Nempty+Nsingleton` is a lower bound on the sum of four root deficits. The ordering is lexicographic `(L,Nempty,M)`, with M evaluated only when L=0. On L=0 projections, all2048 center assignments are optimized exactly subject to GF2 legality using the inherited bad-affine-subspace scorer. The actual unordered bad-pair count is32M. A zero actual deficit saves the full edge list and stops immediately.

There are exactly16 starts: the previously independently checked832-bad-pair source and15 fresh distinct legal constrained projections. Fresh starts use Python `random.Random(20261027091)`: draw each of eleven independent D32 voltages uniformly, derive the two dependent projections, then reject only illegal or duplicate starts. The actual starts and draw count are saved. There is no previous two-coordinate-best start in this batch.

Each start has9 segments of at most30 strict descent moves. Every accepted move follows inspection of the full341-neighbor independent single-coordinate neighborhood. The two dependent projections are recomputed after each mutation. Only strict objective improvement is accepted. Between segments, two distinct independent coordinates are both changed using a separate `std::mt19937_64` stream seeded20261027091; dependent projections are recomputed and proposals rejected until GF2 legal. No phase filter, additional start, other group, or extra coordinate ball is used.

The first source evaluation restores all16 saved full voltages using center mask198. Its dependent baseline values are38=(6,1) and63=(31,1), so the second dependent high bit is explicitly exercised. All1024 saved representative-root radius3 distance entries are compared with short-walk evaluation. This reuses the old independent distance matrix: no source BFS or2048-center enumeration is performed. Four quotient-identity self slots are also checked to contain the constant forms0 and1.

The budget is one worker and180 CPU seconds including preparation and compilation. A budget stop preserves the current start/segment/step/neighbor cursor and best neighbor so far, without committing an incomplete neighborhood or restarting. If no L=0 projection occurs, only the best projection/lower bound is saved, not a purported full graph. Otherwise only the best actual scored graph is retained for the root agent's unique independent final check.
