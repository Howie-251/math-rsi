## Definitions match the criterion

`Uniform`, `Bichromatic`, and `PropertyB` are the usual ones. `singlePairs` is the set of ordered pairs \((e,f)\in E\times E\) with \(e\neq f\) and \(|e\cap f|=1\); `gamma` is its cardinality. `ak k p = 1-(1-p)^k` and `jk k p = \int_0^p (1-y^2)^{k-1}\,dy`. These are definitionally what the audit file inlines.

The sample is the finite model asked for in the stage goal: fair initial colors, independent Bernoulli(\(p\)) eligibility, and a uniform permutation, with total mass `1`. A vertex is processed once, in increasing \(\sigma\)-order, and flips only when it is eligible and some edge through it is initially monochromatic in its own initial color and still disjoint from the flipped set. `Failure` is a monochromatic edge of `finalColor`. `rsFailureProbability` runs that algorithm on `relabel (Fintype.equivFin V) E`. Cardinality, uniformity, and \(\gamma\) are invariant, and a successful `finalColor` is pulled back by \(c\circ f\).

The \(k\ge 2\) argument is uniform in \(k\). The constant \(17\) appears only in the numerical caller.

## Connections that were attacked

The failure cover is the right one. An edge that stays at its initial color has every eligibility bit false. An edge that becomes monochromatic in a new color has a last flipped vertex \(x\) and a witness edge \(g\) with \(f\cap g=\{x\}\). `PairEvent` is exactly that necessary condition, including the ordered constraints \(\sigma z<\sigma x\) on the same-color side of \(f\) and \(\sigma x<\sigma z\) on eligible vertices of \(g\). It is not claimed to be sufficient. `pairEvent_iff_local` sends it to the pivot, the disjoint sets \(f\setminus\{x\}\) and \(g\setminus\{x\}\), and `BeforeAfter`.

`beforeAfter_ratio` is \(a!\,b!/(a+b+1)!\), for disjoint \(A,B\) not containing the pivot, with inactive vertices cancelled. The block embedding is only an internal numbering. `beta_nat` is the same factorial ratio as \(\int_0^1 t^a(1-t)^b\,dt\), proved by a polynomial recurrence. Finite Bernoulli sums pass through the integral because the integrands are continuous. Coordinates factor only because the pivot and the two punctured edges are disjoint; overlapping edges are not treated as independent. The averaged integrand is

\[
\frac p2\Bigl(\frac{1+pt}2\Bigr)^{k-1}\Bigl(\frac{1-pt}2\Bigr)^{k-1}
= 2^{1-2k}\,p\,(1-p^2t^2)^{k-1}.
\]

`jk_rescale` is Mathlib’s substitution \(p\int_0^1 f(pt)\,dt=\int_0^p f\), so one color has probability \(J_k(p)/2^{2k-1}\). The two colors and \(2^{2k-1}=2\cdot(2^{k-1})^2\) give \(\gamma J_k(p)/(2^{k-1})^2\). That is the same constant as \(\gamma J_k(p)/4^{k-1}\).

The residual term is also the right inequality. Given the initial coloring, the monochromatic edges — overlaps included — contribute increasing hit events. `bernoulli_harris` is the product-Bernoulli induction for every \(p\in[0,1]\): the conditional covariance is \(p(1-p)(u_1-u_0)(v_1-v_0)\). Thus the conditional residual probability is at most \(1-a_k(p)^X\). The mean of \(X\) is exactly \(|E|/2^{k-1}\), by linearity. Since \(t\mapsto a^t\) is convex for \(a>0\), Jensen supplies \(\mathbb E[a^X]\ge a^{\mathbb E X}\), which upper-bounds the failure probability. Empty \(E\) is included.

The pilot estimate is strict for the actual \(\gamma(E)\). It uses \(\gamma\le |E|^2\), \(J_{17}(1/17)\le 1/17\), and \(a_{17}(1/17)>1/2\). Then \(a^{|E|/2^{16}}\ge a^2>1/4\) and \(\gamma J/d^2\le 4/17<1/4\).
